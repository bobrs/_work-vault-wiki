# Terminology Freeze

Status: working freeze for Phase H/I preparation  
Content canon status: unset  
Applies to: Telic Field Papers F.1–F.11 and reviews G.1–G.12

This freeze prevents terminology drift while preserving explicit provisional status where the research remains unsettled.

## Freeze levels

```text
FROZEN
PROVISIONAL
QUALIFIED
LINEAGE ONLY
RETIRED
```

- **FROZEN** — use consistently in the next branches.
- **PROVISIONAL** — retain for testing; do not present as settled field terminology.
- **QUALIFIED** — use only with an explicit modifier or scoped definition.
- **LINEAGE ONLY** — preserve in historical notes or essays, not technical specification.
- **RETIRED** — do not use in current architecture.

## Core concepts

| Term | Status | Frozen definition or rule |
|---|---|---|
| **telos** | FROZEN | An end-relative orientation or condition that makes possible states non-equivalent for a center. Do not equate with attractor, metric, reward, or universal purpose. |
| **telic field** | FROZEN | The local, changing structure in which possible states matter differently to a center of standing, including goals, boundaries, protected conditions, avoided states, costs, uncertainty, and possible routes. Not a physical field. |
| **teleological field** | QUALIFIED | Explanatory or essay-language synonym for telic field. Prefer **telic field** in technical prose. |
| **center of standing** | FROZEN | A person, community, role, institution, or affected center whose conditions or consequences must remain represented for the action at issue. The standing type must be qualified where ambiguity matters. |
| **standing** | QUALIFIED | Default: constitutional representation requirement. Use **legal standing**, **operational standing**, **temporal standing**, or **internal representational standing** where applicable. |
| **constitutional standing** | FROZEN | Requirement that a materially affected center or protected condition remain represented before a loop legitimately acts. |
| **operational standing** | FROZEN | Recognized capacity of a role, institution, relational field, or continuing loop to participate in governance. Does not imply moral personhood. |
| **internal representational standing** | FROZEN | Representation of an internal orientation, embodied signal, or temporal state inside person-level governance. Does not create an independent person. |
| **telic projection** | FROZEN | A scoped, attributable, correctable representation of part of a telic field for a particular relation or action. |
| **receiver mirror** | FROZEN | A receiver's current interpretation of a projection or trace. It remains attributable to the receiver until confirmed or otherwise authorized. |
| **projection integrity** | FROZEN | Adequacy of source, scope, evidence status, uncertainty, authority, and correction for the action a projection is asked to govern. |
| **telic integrity** | FROZEN | Preservation of the legitimate reason, protected conditions, and authority for acting through transformation and time. |
| **semantic integrity** | FROZEN | Action-relative preservation of material meaning, source relation, scope, uncertainty, standing, and correction through transformation. |
| **temporal integrity** | FROZEN | Preservation of valid relations among history, authority, consent, state, consequence, succession, correction, and release. |

## Relation and loop concepts

| Term | Status | Frozen definition or rule |
|---|---|---|
| **dyad** | QUALIFIED | Minimum analytic cut for distinct local standing, projection, interpretation, and relational consequence. Not a proven universal atom or isolated pair. |
| **relational telic field** | PROVISIONAL | Emergent end-relative structure of a relation. It can create consequence before it creates legitimate authority. |
| **derived loop** | FROZEN | A recurring organization whose operation or identity depends upon prior, lower, or outer loops. Derived does not mean unreal or inferior. |
| **higher loop / outer loop** | FROZEN | A loop that contains, governs, funds, constrains, receives value from, or otherwise affects a local loop. Must not imply moral superiority. |
| **constitutional substrate** | FROZEN | Material, semantic, relational, institutional, telic, and capacity conditions required for a loop to remain coherent without systematic standing loss or coercive compensation. |
| **telic compatibility** | FROZEN | Ability of a form or loop to preserve intended purpose, standing, authority, correction, and substrate in a receiving field. |
| **telic incompatibility** | FROZEN | Inability to sustain the intended loop without systematic coercion, drift, extraction, or substrate depletion. |
| **telic transplant failure** | FROZEN | Failure of an imported form to establish the intended operative loop in a receiving field. |
| **constitutional substrate mismatch** | FROZEN | Absence or incompatibility of governance, standing, capacity, or repair conditions required by a loop. |
| **teleological graft rejection** | LINEAGE ONLY | Evocative origin metaphor. Avoid in technical or public claims where biological or cultural-purity readings may arise. |
| **constitutional fork** | PROVISIONAL | Lineage-preserving separation of legitimately incompatible paths with explicit allocation of assets, records, standing, and obligations. |
| **dissolution** | FROZEN | Governed end of a loop's recruitment and authority, including residual state, obligations, witness, and bounded aftercare. |
| **aftercare loop** | PROVISIONAL | Narrow successor loop responsible for claims, archives, repair, monitoring, or residual duties after dissolution. |

## Self and time

| Term | Status | Frozen definition or rule |
|---|---|---|
| **constitutional self** | FROZEN | Person-level governance model for several internal orientations, embodied signals, and temporal states under one sovereign person. Not a discovery of internal plurality and not a clinical diagnosis. |
| **person-level sovereignty** | FROZEN | The person remains the primary constitutional unit over internal representations. No internal orientation or model becomes an independent sovereign by default. |
| **executive authority** | FROZEN | Bounded capacity to select or perform action for the person or loop. Executive control is not identical to selfhood. |
| **internal consent** | QUALIFIED | Adequate internal representation and non-erasure for person-level action; not literal contracting among separate persons and not unanimity. |
| **temporal standing** | FROZEN | Requirement that temporally displaced states, successors, or future affected centers remain represented when current action materially constrains what they will inherit. |
| **temporary governor** | QUALIFIED | Explanatory metaphor for present authority over conditions future states will inherit. Not a legal fiduciary classification. |
| **inherited authority** | FROZEN | Authority carried from earlier decisions, consent, roles, or commitments, subject to scope, context, continuity, review, and release. |
| **versioned consent** | FROZEN | Consent state preserved with scope, version, valid time, transaction time, revision, withdrawal, and downstream-use history. |
| **future possibility** | FROZEN | Protected capacity for later states to choose or revise where current uncertainty and irreversibility make option preservation material. Not a duty to preserve every option. |

## Context and semantic trail

| Term | Status | Frozen definition or rule |
|---|---|---|
| **context carrying capacity** | FROZEN | Active capacity of a loop to preserve relevant plurality, contradiction, provenance, uncertainty, and standing at the resolution required for legitimate next action. A profile, not a universal scalar. |
| **effective standing** | FROZEN | Standing actively represented or reliably recoverable in the current decision context. Loss of effective standing does not erase constitutional standing. |
| **semantic trail** | FROZEN | A persistent difference produced or preserved through prior activity that can become meaningful through later uptake and reorganize navigation among possibilities. |
| **semantic field** | QUALIFIED | In this series only: the distributed, revisable ecology of traces, conventions, artifacts, relations, and interpretive practices through which prior distinctions become available to later navigation. Always include or link the scoped definition. |
| **semantic-trail ecology** | PROVISIONAL | Preferred narrow alternative where **semantic field** would create disciplinary ambiguity. Recommended for public technical explanation. |
| **semantic durability** | FROZEN | Persistence and potential reactivation of a trace. Does not imply integrity or legitimacy. |
| **semantic inheritance** | FROZEN | Receipt of language, categories, records, institutions, and traces not created by the receiving center, with continuing rights of reinterpretation, correction, refusal, and release. |
| **semantic witness** | FROZEN | Source and transformation lineage sufficient to inspect how a trace entered action. Does not prove truth or consent. |
| **semantic trajectory** | FROZEN | Versioned sequence by which a source expression becomes extracted, normalized, summarized, retrieved, interpreted, and acted upon. |
| **semantic commons** | FROZEN | Shared semantic resources governed through contribution, custody, access, attribution, correction, privacy, maintenance, and succession. Not unrestricted openness. |
| **semantic enclosure** | PROVISIONAL | Asymmetric control over shared semantic resources that materially removes contributor standing, correction, portability, or viable exit. |
| **semantic pollution** | QUALIFIED | Traces or amplification patterns that materially degrade legitimate orientation. Never use to classify dissent merely because it disrupts consensus. |
| **semantic sedimentation** | LINEAGE ONLY / PROVISIONAL | Metaphor for training or institutional layering that compresses traces and loses direct source visibility. Use only with explanation. |

## Navigation and decision

| Term | Status | Frozen definition or rule |
|---|---|---|
| **polytely** | FROZEN / ESTABLISHED | Presence or interaction of multiple goals or ends. |
| **polytelometry** | PROVISIONAL | Disciplined effort to make relations among several ends, sources, authority, standing, consent, and consequences legible. Not a promise of one quantitative unit. |
| **polytelometric navigation** | FROZEN FOR SERIES | Governance process for admitting, classifying, generating, testing, gating, selecting, witnessing, and revising routes among plural ends. Not a universal decision rule. |
| **field class** | FROZEN | One of: shared, compatible, conditional, conflicting, protected, unresolved, missing-standing, or released. |
| **protected condition / protected field** | FROZEN | Condition unavailable for ordinary tradeoff under current authority. Must carry source, scope, temporal validity, and review path. |
| **missing-standing field** | FROZEN | A materially affected center or condition is not adequately represented. Do not encode as a zero weight. |
| **route** | FROZEN | Candidate sequence of actions, conditions, cost bearers, gates, review, reversibility, exit, and residual state—not endpoint alone. |
| **cost-bearer map** | FROZEN | Explicit representation of who bears direct cost, risk, labor, uncertainty, surveillance, opportunity loss, repair, lock-in, and residual harm. |
| **no-decision state** | FROZEN | Explicit result such as defer, refuse, pause, narrow, escalate, fork, or declare no adequate route. It still carries consequences. |
| **representation fairness** | PROVISIONAL | Quality of source recognition, minority preservation, correction opportunity, uncertainty visibility, and resistance to order or articulation bias. |
| **decision witness** | FROZEN | Source, rule, route, dissent, authority, action, and consequence record sufficient for later review and repair. |

| Term | Status | Frozen definition or rule |

| **non-sovereign model** | FROZEN | Model whose role, data access, tools, and authority remain external, scoped, inspectable, correctable, revocable, and contestable. |

| **model plurality** | FROZEN DISTINCTION | Several model instances or agents. It never implies standing plurality by itself. |
| **human re-entry** | FROZEN | Practical intervention with source access, semantic trajectory, time, competence, authority, and viable ability to pause, alter, reverse, or escalate an action. |

## Training governance

| Term | Status | Frozen definition or rule |
|---|---|---|
| **training recruitment** | FROZEN | Transfer of semantic, human, material, and institutional capacity into a model-development outer loop. |
| **training authority** | FROZEN | Legal, contractual, individual, collective, community, public, fiduciary, or other legitimate basis governing a source's use in training. Consent is one authority class. |
| **consentfully trained model** | PROVISIONAL | Qualified, auditable training-lineage profile. Never a binary badge and never a claim that every source used individual opt-in consent. |
| **source standing** | FROZEN | Material source interests in privacy, attribution, benefit, community authority, confidentiality, correction, withdrawal, restriction, and harmful use. |
| **preference provenance** | FROZEN | Population, recruitment, task, policy, alternatives, disagreement, aggregation, adjudication, protected objections, and gaps underlying preference training. |
| **restriction propagation** | FROZEN | Preservation of license, consent, authority, community, privacy, withdrawal, and benefit obligations through collection, transformation, training, derivation, and succession. |
| **withdrawal ladder** | FROZEN | Distinct states from collection stop and source deletion through future exclusion, retrieval blocking, approximate unlearning, retraining, and model retirement. |
| **unlearning** | QUALIFIED / ESTABLISHED | Technical removal claim. Always state method, evaluation, threat model, retained utility, and residual uncertainty. Never use as synonym for source deletion. |
| **synthetic-data lineage** | FROZEN | Generator, version, source lineage, policy, filtering, proportion, recursive depth, and contamination/diversity evaluation for generated training data. |
| **benefit governance** | FROZEN | Rules for attribution, compensation, shared infrastructure, public or collective benefit, governance rights, and dispute. Benefit does not purchase unlimited authority. |
| **training/operation separation** | FROZEN INVARIANT | Training-lineage legitimacy and deployment legitimacy are separate profiles; neither substitutes for the other. |
