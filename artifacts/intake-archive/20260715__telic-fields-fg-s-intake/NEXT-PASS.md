## Gate decision

At the end of HI-0, decide whether:

- I.1 and H.1 proceed in parallel;
- terminology requires revision;
- one record type should be removed;
- the architecture remains too burdensome;

## Governing invariant

> A representation may gain action power only through a visible chain of source, standing, authority, consent where applicable, witness, correction, and exit.
