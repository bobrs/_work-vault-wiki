# J.3 — Observed External Exercise, Governance Handoff, and Pilot Admission

## Purpose

Move from internally generated independence to actual organizational and human independence.

J.3 should decide whether the J.2 release candidate may enter a bounded external pilot.

## Required outputs

1. external security review and signed disposition;
2. external privacy review and signed disposition;
3. assistive-technology user exercise;
4. observed real multi-party scheduling trial;
5. authenticated identity and role-lifecycle profile;
6. genuinely separated operational and release-key custody;
7. networked queue and service fault exercise;
8. incident and escalation ownership map;
9. governance handoff and operator acceptance record;
10. repository and wiki ingestion verification;
11. roadmap metadata reconciliation record;
12. pilot-admission decision witness.

## Required exercise

At least three real participants should provide scoped scheduling projections. At least two participants must retain independent correction routes. The trial should include one denied route, one authorized route, one observed consequence, and one correction that changes a later outcome.

No participant should be required to disclose protected source content publicly.

## Review independence

The security, privacy, accessibility, and governance reviewers must not all be the implementation author or operate under one undifferentiated role.

Reviewer findings should preserve:

```text
reviewer identity and authority
scope
evidence
severity
recommended disposition
implementer response
residual risk
acceptance or rejection
```

## Custody requirement

Release and operational authority should require separately held keys or approval credentials. A single local filesystem must not hold all active private authority material.

## Pilot-admission outcomes

```text
ADMIT_BOUNDED_PILOT
ADMIT_WITH_CONDITIONS
REMEDIATE_AND_REVIEW
REJECT
UNKNOWN
CONTESTED
```

## Shared gate

J.3 passes only if:

- external review is real rather than simulated;
- participant correction is exercised by real affected centers;
- review findings can block admission;
- key custody is organizationally separated;
- the external verifier reproduces the release and witness checks;
- open privacy and accessibility conditions have named owners;
- repository and wiki ingestion are independently verified;
- the D/E metadata inconsistency is superseded in the active project record;
- the admission claim remains bounded to the observed pilot.
