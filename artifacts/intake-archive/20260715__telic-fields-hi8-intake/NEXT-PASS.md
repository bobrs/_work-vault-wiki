## Shared gate

HI-9 passes only if:

- public availability is not treated as consent;
- legal or licensed use does not erase source standing;
- one consent event does not authorize every later transformation or deployment;
- preference aggregation does not become collective legitimacy;
- synthetic data does not become source-free;
- deletion remains distinct from unlearning and downstream repair;
- derivative-model lineage remains visible;
- H.9 explains consentful training without claiming perfect provenance or retroactive purification.
