## F.10 result

Semantic polytelometry is defined as a bounded architecture through which language models preserve and transform plural projections long enough for their owners to inspect, correct, coordinate, and navigate them.

The paper develops:

- field, projection, trail, context, mirror, and route separation;
- explicit model roles;
- model constitution and provider teloi;
- source–model–provider–institution layers;
- epistemic status and uncertainty;
- constitutional retrieval;
- semantic-trajectory tracking;
- participant recognition;
- sycophancy and false attunement;
- multi-agent and mediation boundaries;
- tool-use and agentic-planning gates;
- human re-entry;
- contestability, recourse, breach, and repair;
- a minimum viable architecture and session record.
