# Frame-Anchored Consent Protocol (FACP) v0.1

## Purpose

A cross-domain protocol for evaluating whether an interaction, decision, or system supports **meaningful consent 🝁** under real conditions.

Grounded in Quantum Invariants primitives and composites.

---

## Core Principle

> Legibility 🝮 → Consent 🝁 → Trust

If legibility fails, consent degrades.
If consent degrades, trust becomes unstable.

---

## Protocol Steps

### 1. 🝚 Boundary Definition (P1)
**Question:** What is the system boundary?
- What is inside vs outside?
- What crossing is occurring?

**Failure Signals:**
- Blurred responsibility
- Unclear ownership

---

### 2. 🜹 Witness Capacity (P2)
**Question:** Who is the agent, and do they have capacity?
- Can they understand consequences?
- Can they act meaningfully?

**Failure Signals:**
- Power asymmetry
- Cognitive overload

---

### 3. 🝮 Legibility Check (P4)
**Question:** Is the interaction legible enough?
- Can the affected party restate implications?
- Is reasoning traceable?

**Failure Signals:**
- Opaque systems
- Hidden assumptions

---

### 4. 🝁 Consent Gate (P3)
**Question:** Is consent present and valid?
- Is it explicit, scoped, and revocable?

**Failure Signals:**
- Implied or coerced agreement
- No exit path

---

### 5. ↩️ Reversibility (P8)
**Question:** Can this be undone?
- What is the rollback path?

**Failure Signals:**
- Irreversible commitments
- Lock-in dynamics

---

### 6. 🝖 Governance Scaling (P9)
**Question:** Does control match impact?
- What is the blast radius?
- Who bears downside?

**Failure Signals:**
- High impact, low oversight
- Over-control of low-risk systems

---

### 7. 🝳 Feedback Loops (P6)
**Question:** What loops reinforce this system?
- Are errors amplified or dampened?

**Failure Signals:**
- Runaway dynamics
- Delayed correction

---

### 8. ⚖️ Ledger Check (P5)
**Question:** Are flows accounted for?
- Who pays cost vs gains benefit?

**Failure Signals:**
- Hidden debt
- Externalized harm

---

### 9. 🪞 Comparator Check (P10)
**Question:** What defines success?
- Is the comparator explicit?
- Who can revise it?

**Failure Signals:**
- Hidden metrics
- Sacralized KPIs

---

## Composite Evaluation

### C6 — Consent Gradient
Evaluate:
- Capacity (P2)
- Legibility (P4)
- Reversibility (P8)
- Governance (P9)

If any degrade → consent quality degrades

---

## Output States

- **Green:** Consent structurally supported
- **Yellow:** Partial consent (constraints present)
- **Red:** Consent not meaningful (system failure)

---

## Usage

### Human
- Use as reflective checklist
- Apply in conversations, decisions, systems

### Machine
- Anchor each step to P# / C#
- Generate structured diagnostics

---

## Final Compression

> No legibility 🝮 → no consent 🝁 → no trust

> With structure:
> Observe 🜹 → Evaluate 🪞 → Consent 🝁 → Iterate 🝳
