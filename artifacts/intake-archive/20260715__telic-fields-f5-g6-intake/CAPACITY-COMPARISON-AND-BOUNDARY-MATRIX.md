# Context Carrying Capacity — Comparison and Boundary Matrix

Status: draft  
Content canon status: unset

| Neighboring concept | What it already explains | What context carrying capacity must add |
|---|---|---|
| Requisite variety | Regulatory response capacity relative to disturbance variety | Standing, consent, authority, privacy, and stop conditions |
| Good regulator | Need for an adequate model of the regulated system | Constitutional constraints and affected-center representation |
| Bounded rationality | Decision under finite information and computation | Who loses standing under satisficing and what must remain protected |
| Working memory | Limited active human representation | Distributed, recoverable, and externally scaffolded capacity |
| Cognitive load | Task and presentation demands on limited processing | Preservation of relevant plurality, not ease alone |
| Information bottleneck | Compression preserving target-relevant information | Legitimacy of the relevance target and protected omissions |
| Distributed cognition | Capacity across people, artifacts, and time | Mapping standing, authority, correction, and consequence |
| Common ground | Sufficient mutual understanding for communication | Known disagreement, absent centers, privacy, and legitimacy |
| Organizational information processing | Capacity or demand reduction under uncertainty | Constitutional routing and standing-aware organization design |
| Sensemaking | Actionable interpretation under ambiguity | Protected standing through meaning collapse and re-foundation |
| Long-context language models | Nominal and effective machine context use | Constitutional retrieval, source integrity, and authority degradation |
| Graceful degradation | Preserving essential function under partial failure | Reducing action authority when context becomes inadequate |

## Governing distinctions

```text
storage ≠ active context
input length ≠ effective use
information volume ≠ relevant difference
shared context ≠ legitimate context
compression ≠ neutral preservation
low load ≠ adequate standing
regulatory variety ≠ moral authority
effective standing ≠ constitutional standing
```
