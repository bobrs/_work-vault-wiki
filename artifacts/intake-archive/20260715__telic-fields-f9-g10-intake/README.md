## F.9 result

Polytelometric navigation is defined as a governance process for movement among plural ends.

It does not replace optimization, MCDA, robust planning, argumentation, negotiation, social choice, or deliberation.

It governs:

- which ends enter;
- whose standing they represent;
- what is protected;
- what remains unresolved;
- which routes are generated;
- who bears cost;
- what consent and authority are required;
- when the loop should stop.
