## H.4 — The Present Is Not the Whole Timeline

Write the fourth public reader page.

Develop:

- present authority as temporary governance;
- continuity without sameness;
- future standing without present consent;
- commitments as bounded transfers of authority;
- promises without permanent captivity;
- stale projections;
- option preservation;
- irreversibility;
- revision, release, repair, and succession.

### Public governing lines

> **The present self is a temporary governor of conditions that later states will inherit.**

> **Continuity is not sameness. It is answerability across change.**

## Shared gate

- irreversibility increases future-standing review;
- successors inherit obligations without inheriting unlimited sovereignty;
- H.4 explains revision without treating every change as betrayal.
