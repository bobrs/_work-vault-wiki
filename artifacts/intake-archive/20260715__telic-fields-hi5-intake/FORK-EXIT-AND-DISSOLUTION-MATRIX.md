# Fork, Exit, and Dissolution Matrix

Status: candidate pre-production  
Content canon status: unset

| State | Shared history | Future authority | Records | Obligations | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Corrected single loop | Preserved | Renewed within one loop | Corrected and versioned | Continue under revised scope | Renewed or redistributed |
| Narrowed loop | Preserved | Reduced | Unneeded access removed | Residual obligations retained | Reduced |
| Fork | Preserved as lineage | Separate by branch | Shared past; separate future | Allocated explicitly | Recruited separately |
| Exit | Preserved | Ends for departing center except residual duties | Portable, corrected, deleted, or restricted | Valid residual duties remain | Future recruitment ends |
| Transfer | Preserved | Revalidated by operation | Custody and access declared | Attached obligations transfer | Support grants reviewed |
| Release | Preserved | Ends for released purpose or contribution | Bounded witness remains | Reliance and repair identified | Recruitment ends |
| Dissolution | Preserved in final witness | Ends | Custody, export, restriction, and deletion assigned | Survive until fulfilled, transferred, repaired, or released | Released |
| Abandonment | Often fragmented | Unclear | Orphaned | Unassigned | Informally captured |

## Fork test

A fork is legitimate only if it identifies:

```text
departing and remaining centers
shared lineage
future authority
record custody
asset allocation
obligations
privacy
shared infrastructure
transition
repair
```

## Dissolution test

A dissolution is complete only when:

```text
future support recruitment has ended
assets are assigned
restricted data has custody or deletion
obligations are assigned
unresolved harms remain visible
repair funds are allocated
successor authority is explicit or absent
public notice is complete
final witness exists
```

## Governing principle

> Ending the form may be necessary to release the substrate while preserving what the loop still owes.
