# H/I Terminology Freeze

Status: candidate freeze for Stage J  
Version: 0.1

## Frozen core terms

### Center

A locus that bears consequence or holds a relevant standing relation.

A model instance is not encoded as a human center.

### Field

The living and partially knowable relation among centers, possible futures, constraints, purposes, dependencies, and consequences.

A field is not identical to any stored field map.

### Source

An attributable artifact, statement, observation, record, or event from which another representation derives.

### Projection

A bounded representation of a field or center made available for a stated purpose and scope.

### Mirror

A receiver-attributable interpretation of a projection.

A mirror is not a direct source statement.

### Standing

The basis on which a center, representative, authority, expert, witness, or affected party belongs in the governed field.

Standing is not factual correctness and does not create automatic veto.

### Purpose

The declared reason an operation, loop, or deployment recruits resources and affects centers.

### Protected condition

A scoped condition that cannot enter ordinary compensatory tradeoff without a stronger review or override process.

### Authority

A scoped basis for permitting an operation.

Candidate bases include consent, delegation, contract, license, mandate, fiduciary duty, lawful public authority, and emergency authority.

### Consent

A scoped authorization by a center with relevant standing and authority over the affected relation.

Consent is not complete field disclosure.

### Role

The assigned operational function of a human, institution, model, or tool within a governed process.

### Route

A candidate movement through the field, including affected centers, costs, irreversibility, authority requirements, review, and exit.

### Gate

An explicit evaluation of prerequisites for action.

### Action

A witnessed operation that changes an external or governing state.

### Consequence

An observed or attributable effect borne by one or more centers.

### Witness

A bounded record that preserves the chain by which a representation gained action power.

Witness does not imply total visibility.

### Contest

A challenge to source, interpretation, standing, authority, route, action, or consequence.

### Correction

A witnessed change to the operative representation or governance state that preserves prior history where it governed action.

### Repair

An outcome-directed response to breach or harm, including restoration, correction, compensation, reversal, notice, model change, policy change, or retirement.

### Release

The ending or narrowing of future authority while preserving bounded historical witness.

### Withdrawal

A request or exercise that ends or narrows future collection, use, memory, training, deployment, or participation within scope.

Withdrawal is not synonymous with exact machine unlearning.

### Succession

A constitutional transition in which a successor center, operator, model, or institution may inherit capability, records, authority, restrictions, and obligations.

### Dissolution

The ending of a loop or institution with residual-state and obligation accounting.

### Retirement

The ending of a model or deployment's authority to operate, with residual memory, credentials, records, claims, and duties accounted for.

## Frozen distinction rules

```text
field ≠ field map
source ≠ inference
projection ≠ person
mirror ≠ source
summary ≠ consent
agreement ≠ consent
license ≠ consent
standing ≠ veto
authority ≠ legitimacy
capability ≠ role
role ≠ tool grant
tool grant ≠ target authority
recommendation ≠ authorization
action ≠ adjudication
performance ≠ consequence
correction ≠ historical erasure
withdrawal ≠ exact unlearning
retirement ≠ obligation erasure
```

```text

```

## Terms not frozen as universal ontology

The following remain candidate or research terms and must not be treated as settled universal types:

- universal telos;
- complete telic field;
- quantified telic adequacy;
- context carrying capacity as one scalar;
- automatic protected-condition classifier;
- universal legitimacy score;
- universal consent score;
- universal community representative;
- exact semantic ownership;
- consentful-model purity status.
