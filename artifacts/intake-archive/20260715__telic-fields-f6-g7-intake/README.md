## F.6 result

Temporal standing is defined as the requirement that temporally displaced states or successors remain represented in present decisions that materially constrain the conditions they will inherit.

The paper develops:

- continuity without perfect sameness;
- present authority as temporary governance;
- future possibility as a protected condition;
- commitment as temporal recruitment;
- inherited-authority review;
- versioned consent;
- temporal witness;
- identity as a continuity protocol;
- personal, institutional, and model succession;
- release from prior teloi;
- temporal breach and repair.

## G.7 result

The review grounds temporal standing beside:
