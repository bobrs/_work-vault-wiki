# Decision-Method Adapter Matrix

Status: candidate pre-production  
Content canon status: unset

Established decision methods enter the Telic Field architecture through declared adapters.

| Adapter | Permitted input | Permitted output | Required disclosures | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pareto | Decision-ready routes and criteria | Dominance relation and nondominated set | Admitted routes, criteria, excluded conditions | Does not select or legitimate |
| MCDA | Routes, criteria, weights, thresholds | Scores, rankings, sensitivity | Weight source, thresholds, uncertainty | Protected conditions remain outside ordinary compensation |
| Outranking | Routes and preference thresholds | Pairwise outranking and incomparability | Veto thresholds and criterion source | Veto rule requires authority |
| Lexicographic | Ordered criteria | Route comparison under explicit priority | Priority source and review path | Priority is not neutral |
| Robustness | Routes and scenarios | Performance across uncertainty | Scenario provenance and failure thresholds | Robust for whom must remain visible |
| Satisficing | Minimum conditions and routes | Routes meeting thresholds | Threshold authority and unmet conditions | Minimums may conceal excluded standing |
| Argumentation | Claims, evidence, attacks, supports | Defensible and unresolved positions | Source, authority, burden rule | Winning argument is not consent |
| Negotiation | Participant projections and concessions | Candidate agreement | Representation and consent scope | Agreement by proxy requires authority |
| Deliberation | Participant positions and route options | Revised understanding or recommendation | Minority preservation and facilitation rule | Consensus is not consent |
| Policy rule | Versioned policy and facts | Eligibility or routing result | Policy version, exception, appeal | Model interpretation is not adjudication |
| Human judgment | Full decision record | Selection or no-decision | Human authority and available alternatives | Human presence is not meaningful control by itself |
| Model recommendation | Structured field and declared rule | Proposed route | Model role, source, uncertainty | Recommendation has no authorization by default |

## Adapter sequence

```text
admission
→ protected-condition review
→ decision-ready route set
→ declared adapter
→ comparison result
→ deliberation or selection
→ governance gate
```

An adapter must never be placed before standing and protected-condition admission merely because the software expects a complete numeric table.
