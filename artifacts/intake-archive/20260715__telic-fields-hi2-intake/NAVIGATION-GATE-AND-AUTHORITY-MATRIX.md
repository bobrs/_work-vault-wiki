# Navigation, Gate, and Authority Matrix

Status: candidate pre-production  
Content canon status: unset

| Object or operation | What it contributes | What it does not authorize |
|---|---|---|
| Field classification | Current relation among projected items | Final truth about the source field |
| Route generation | Candidate paths, stages, forks, or exits | Consent, authorization, or execution |
| Pareto analysis | Nondominated route set | Fairness, legitimacy, or final choice |
| MCDA | Comparison under declared criteria and weights | Authority over protected conditions or absent centers |
| Argumentation | Claims, support, attack, and unresolved dispute | Consent or standing |
| Deliberation | Shared reasoning and possible revision | Unanimity or automatic consent |
| Protected-condition review | Whether ordinary tradeoff is unavailable under current authority | Eternal or universal absoluteness |
| Cost-bearer map | Who bears labor, risk, privacy loss, lock-in, or repair | A monetary conversion of every cost |
| Governance gate | Whether a route may proceed under current rules | Creation of missing authority |
| Model recommendation | Model-generated route preference | Authorization or execution |
| Human judgment | Contextual selection under human authority | Legitimacy when source, standing, or alternatives remain hidden |
| Decision witness | Event-linked record of source, routes, gates, action, and consequence | Truth beyond the witness scope |
| Consequence review | Comparison of expected and observed effects | Silent rewrite of the prior decision |

## Gate composition

```text
standing
authority
consent
capacity
privacy
temporal validity
protected conditions
decision rule
tool use
human re-entry
stop
```

No gate dimension may disappear inside one composite score.

## Governing distinctions

```text
efficient ≠ legitimate
nondominated ≠ authorized
considered ≠ consented
recommended ≠ selected
selected ≠ authorized
authorized ≠ executed
executed ≠ successful
witnessed ≠ justified
```

## Hard boundary

> A route becomes actionable only after the system has shown what it preserves, what it sacrifices, who bears the sacrifice, which authority permits it, and what can still stop it.
