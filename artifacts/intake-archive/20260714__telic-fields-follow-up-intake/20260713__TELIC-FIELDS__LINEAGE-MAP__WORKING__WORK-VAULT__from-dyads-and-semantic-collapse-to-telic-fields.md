# Lineage Map: From Dyads and Semantic Collapse to Telic Fields

## Inline summary

The Telic Field Papers did not begin with the word *teleology*. They emerged from a long-running body of work on dyads, consent, witness, loops, semantics, identity, continuity, context, and repair.

The origin conversation supplied a new organizing distinction:

> **Centers of standing inhabit local telic fields.  
> Dyads allow those fields to meet.  
> Semantics carries compressed orientation.  
> Witness carries context through time.  
> Loops make the relation durable.  
> Higher systems compose the pattern across scale.**

This document maps that emergence without rewriting its ancestry.

Earlier work should be treated as precursor, substrate, echo, or structural dependency only where the source supports that relation. The terms **telic field**, **polytelometry**, **semantic polytelometry**, **context carrying capacity**, and **teleological graft rejection** belong first to the 2026-07-13 origin session unless an earlier source is later found.

This map has five jobs:

1. preserve the provenance of the 2026-07-13 emergence;

# 2. Lineage evidence classes

Every lineage edge should be assigned one of these classes.

## 2.1 Explicit precursor

The earlier artifact directly states a claim, structure, or term that the later work visibly extends.

Use when the dependency is textual and clear.

## 2.2 Structural precursor

The earlier artifact contains a model or distinction that later becomes a component of the new architecture, even though the later term is absent.

Use when the relation is strong but interpretive.

## 2.3 Conceptual echo

The earlier artifact resonates with the later structure but is not required to derive it.

Use when similarity is meaningful but lineage is uncertain.

## 2.4 Implementation precursor

The earlier artifact supplies technical machinery later used to implement the conceptual model.

## 2.5 Ethical precursor

The earlier artifact supplies a constraint, standing rule, consent rule, or non-sovereignty principle later inherited by the new work.

## 2.6 Retrospective bridge

The relation became visible only after the 2026-07-13 synthesis.

A retrospective bridge must never be described as though the earlier source already used or intended the later formulation.

## 2.7 Descendant

A document, site, protocol, model, or implementation derived after the origin session.

# 4. Emergence sequence inside the root session

The immediate lineage unfolded in this order.

## 4.1 Attractors

Opening question:

> Are attractors fundamentally teleological objects?

The first distinction separated dynamical convergence from purpose.

**Resulting distinction**

- attractor: a form of convergence;
- telos: an end-relative condition;
- teleology: organization or explanation in relation to ends.

**Lineage effect**

This provided the first language for distinguishing what a system says it serves from what its correction mechanisms preserve.

---

## 4.2 Declared and operative teloi

The Facebook example exposed divergence among:

- declared purpose;
- economic purpose;
- encoded objective;
- operative correction;
- emergent system behavior.

**Resulting branch**

Teleological incoherence.

**Key diagnostic**

> Which objective is allowed to sacrifice the others?

---

## 4.3 Polytelometric navigation

The recognition followed that the work was not merely teleological analysis, but navigation among many ends.

**Resulting terms**

- polytelometry;
- polytelometric navigation.

**Key distinction**

> Optimization asks how to reach the objective.  
> Polytelometric navigation asks which objectives are present, what authority they possess, and whether movement toward them remains legitimate.

---

## 4.4 Teleological terrain and semantic trails

The conversation then distinguished:

- teleological field as terrain;
- intelligence as navigation;
- semantics as durable trail.

This was later refined away from a single universal field toward locally indexed telic fields.

**Resulting branch**

Semantic fields as durable telic trails.

**Key warning**

> When the path is mistaken for the terrain, inherited meaning becomes destiny.

---

## 4.5 Local telic fields and dyadic atoms

The user simplified the origin:

> It all is assembled with atoms of dyads. Each with their own sovereign standing, and telic field.

The architecture became local and compositional.

**Resulting structure**

\[
A(T_A) \;\; 🜁 \;\; B(T_B)
\]

**Key claim**

> Individual standing grounds local telic significance.  
> The 🜁 dyad is the atomic unit through which sovereign telic fields enter shared reality.

---

## 4.6 Internal telic field and simplified calculation

The user identified two forks:

1. internal telic field;
2. simplified telic field calculation for downstream dependents.

**Resulting distinctions**

- full field versus usable projection;
- calculation versus projection;
- source field versus downstream model;
- field breach, projection breach, and projection-integrity breach.

**Key claim**

> Much of intelligence consists of estimating what matters to centers of standing, acting, witnessing the response, and recalculating.

---

## 4.7 Constitutional self

The fractal resolution increased through internal plurality.

Internal Family Systems and other parts-based approaches were recognized as adjacent evidence for a more general claim:

> A person-level telic field emerges through relationships among constitutional elements, each carrying local telic standing.

**Resulting structure**

- constitutional element;
- local internal standing;
- internal 🜁 dyad;
- person-level sovereignty;
- executive authority;
- constitutional legitimacy;
- internal consent;
- internal breach and repair.

**Key claim**

> The self is not a telos. It is a constitution through which many local teloi learn whether they can inhabit one life together.

---

## 4.8 Context carrying capacity

The user identified context fragility as a sensitive fork.

**Resulting term**

Context carrying capacity.

**Key claim**

> No element, person, or system can hold the full telic field of everything it depends upon.

**Key risk**

What falls outside active context can lose effective standing without losing constitutional standing.

---

## 4.9 Temporal stitching

The architecture then expanded across time:

- present and past selves;
- present and future selves;
- founders and successors;
- promises and later inheritors;
- internal elements living in different temporal terrains.

**Resulting terms**

- temporal dyad;
- temporal standing;
- mirror;

**Key claim**

> Witness carries context through time.

---

## 4.10 Semantic polytelometry with LLMs

The implementation bridge emerged:

> Math is not the language. Semantics is.

LLMs were recognized as capable of comparing multiple expressed telic projections before those projections are reduced to metrics.

**Resulting term**

Semantic polytelometry.

**Legitimate role**

- cartographer;
- comparator;
- translator;
- ambiguity detector;
- compression witness.

**Prohibited sovereignty**

The model may not own or declare the true field.

---

## 4.11 Consentfully trained models

The final bridge connected interpretive authority to model provenance.

**Resulting invariant**

> Consentful provenance in. Consentful interpretation out.

**Stronger claim**

> A system cannot consentfully map telic fields while its own field remains hidden, unaccountable, and non-consensual.
