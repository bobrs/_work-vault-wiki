# Coherent Motion Under Uncertainty

This essay reframes manifestation as a problem of trajectory, not outcome.

You do not create the field. You do not summon the wave. You create your trajectory.

Stochastic, outcome-agnostic viewpoints such as:
- Trajectory beats outcome
- Coherence is success
- Adaptation is invariant

reduce internal friction and stabilize behavior. Consistency produces signal. Signal entrains systems.

Magic is what stochastic self-trust feels like from the inside.

Remain coherent while the world moves.
