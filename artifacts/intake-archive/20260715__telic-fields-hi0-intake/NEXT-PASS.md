# HI-1 — Provenance and Public Primer

## I.1 — Provenance, Event, and Correction Specification

Produce:

1. a Telic Field profile of W3C PROV;
2. entity, activity, and agent mappings;
3. bitemporal event rules;
4. correction and supersession semantics;
5. descendant-impact and propagation records;
6. protected omission;
7. selective-disclosure witness views;
8. portable identifiers;
9. independent participant export;
10. negative and adversarial conformance cases.

### Required technical demonstrations

- a correction that reaches a derived mirror, route, and decision witness;
- a correction that cannot reach one descendant and records the gap;
- an expired authority that remains historically visible but cannot authorize a new action;
- a protected restriction verified without disclosing its private source content;
- a model-generated inference that never becomes direct evidence through repeated reuse.

## H.1 — What Matters Before the System Acts

Write the first public primer using Anchored Cadence.

The reader should understand the framework through six questions:

1. What matters?
2. What was actually shared?
3. What did the system infer?
4. Who may act?
5. Who bears the consequence?
6. How can the decision be corrected or ended?

### Required public elements

- landing-page-ready opening;
- Example A in ordinary prose;
- concise explanation of field, projection, mirror, standing, route, witness, and correction;
- one simple diagram;
- prominent non-claims;
- transition to the dyadic and AI examples;
- link map into the research papers.

### Public-language constraint

The first screen should not require the term `polytelometry`.

Introduce the term only after the reader understands the problem it names.

## Shared gate

HI-1 passes only if:

- the public primer accurately reflects the technical correction model;
- protected omission is technically demonstrated and publicly explainable;
- correction does not silently rewrite history;
- provenance is not described as truth or legitimacy;
- the witness can be exported independently;
- the technical record remains proportional for low-stakes use.
