## What this does

We look at your environment the way a real incident would unfold:
- where someone could get in
- what they could reach next
- and which weaknesses matter most

No jargon. No scare tactics. Just a clear, believable picture of where you’re exposed and what to fix first.

---

## What you get

### 1) Exposure Overview
- Key systems in use
- Shared access patterns
- External sharing and overexposed areas
- Places where access may be broader than intended

### 2) Likely Attack Paths
Presented in plain English:
- how a compromised account could spread access
- how shared files or links could expose sensitive information
- where stale accounts, loose permissions, or connected apps create risk
- what an actual incident would most likely touch first

### 3) Scenario Review
We walk through a small number of realistic scenarios, such as:
- “If this inbox is compromised, what becomes reachable?”
- “If this shared folder link leaks, what else is exposed?”
- “If an old account is still active, what could it still access?”

### 4) Fix-First Plan
- Top 5 immediate fixes
- Top 5 structural improvements
- What can safely wait
