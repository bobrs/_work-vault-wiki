# ContinuityOffice
## Services & Engagement Model

**Helping organizations stay in control as they grow.**

ContinuityOffice provides clarity, control, and continuity across your systems — from immediate fixes to long-term structural integrity.

---

# Layer 1 — Clarity & Control (Immediate Engagements)

**Fast, practical engagements to understand what’s happening and fix what matters.**

These are the entry point for most organizations.

## 1. What’s Actually Going On? Audit

A clear, simple review of your files, permissions, and system setup.

**You get:**
- Who has access to what
- Where things are messy or overexposed
- Top risks and top fixes

**Best for:**
- Small teams (5–30 people)
- Growing organizations that feel disorganized

---

## 2. Don’t Wait Till It Happens

A practical simulation of where your environment is exposed and how an incident would likely unfold.

**You get:**
- Exposure overview
- Likely attack paths (plain English)
- Realistic scenarios ("what would happen if...")
- Fix-first plan

**Best for:**
- 20–100 person teams
- Organizations that want to get ahead of risk

---

## 3. Post-Breach Clarity & Control

Understand what happened and make sure it doesn’t happen again.

**You get:**
- Reconstruction of how the issue occurred
- Identification of remaining exposure
- Clear, prioritized remediation plan

**Best for:**
- Organizations that have experienced a security incident
- Teams that no longer trust their current setup

---

# Layer 2 — Structural Reset & Ongoing Support

**Move from reactive fixes to a stable, understandable system.**

## Clean + Control Reset

A structured cleanup of your systems so your team can operate with clarity and confidence.

**Includes:**
- File and system structure redesign
- Permission cleanup and boundary tightening
- Simple usage guidelines for your team
- Documentation for onboarding and consistency

---

## Ongoing Continuity Support (Optional)

Light ongoing support to maintain clarity and prevent drift.

**Includes:**
- Periodic reviews
- Access and structure checks
- Ongoing guidance as your systems evolve

---

# Layer 3 — Enterprise & Platform Integration

**For organizations that need deeper visibility, governance, and continuity across time.**

## Operational Clarity Layer

A structured view of how your systems actually operate.

**Includes:**
- System mapping (tools, flows, dependencies)
- Access and data flow visibility
- Identification of hidden complexity and risk
- Recommendations for structural improvements

---

## Continuity Office Implementation

Establish a formal Continuity function within your organization.

**Focus areas:**
- Intent persistence
- Consent continuity
- Legibility across systems
- Reversibility of decisions
- Power-proportionate governance

---

# Layer 4 — Training & Enablement

**Build internal capability so your team stays in control over time.**

## Workshops & Training

Practical sessions for founders, operators, and teams.

**Topics include:**
- Staying organized and secure as you grow
- Understanding access and permissions
- Avoiding system drift
- Using AI tools safely and effectively

---

## Operator Playbooks

Simple, actionable guides tailored to your organization.

**Focus:**
- Day-to-day clarity
- Repeatable practices
- Consistent decision-making

---

# How it fits together

Most organizations start with a **Clarity & Control engagement**, then move into deeper support as needed.

This creates a natural progression:

1. See what’s actually going on
2. Fix what matters
3. Stabilize your systems
4. Build long-term continuity

# Core Principles

ContinuityOffice is built on a simple foundation:

- **Clear boundaries** — know what can be accessed
- **Intentional use** — know what is allowed and why
- **Visible operations** — know what is actually happening
