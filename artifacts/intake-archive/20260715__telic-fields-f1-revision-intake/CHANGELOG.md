## Material changes

### Terminology boundaries added

The paper now distinguishes the proposed framework from:

- Paul Taylor's teleological centers of life;
- Talcott Parsons's telic system;
- reversal theory's telic and paratelic states;
- linguistic telicity;
- computational telic states and telic controllability;
- established polytely.

### Technical vocabulary revised

- `simplified telic field calculation` is preserved as origin-session lineage language.
- `telic projection estimation` is adopted for current technical use.
- `telic integrity` is used instead of `teleological integrity` in technical framing.

### Novelty posture narrowed

The paper now states that it proposes:

> a new composition and vocabulary for a known family of problems

It does not claim to have discovered:

- goal-directedness;
- plural goals;
- internal multiplicity;
- relational emergence;
- goal-shaped state representation;
- a new physical field.

### Semantic claim narrowed

The universal-sounding claim that semantics simply *is* compressed orientation is now bounded:

> In consequential coordination, semantics often carries compressed orientation among centers and downstream processes.

The shorter aphorism remains a candidate formulation rather than an established universal theory.

### Paratelic and atelic value protected

The paper now explicitly includes:

- play;
- presence;
- rest;
- curiosity without predetermined completion;
- release from inherited purpose;
- the right not to be continually optimized.

### Standing lineage deepened

The paper now acknowledges Paul Taylor's teleological-center-of-life lineage and separates possible grounds for:

- biological standing;
- experiential standing;
- relational standing;
- institutional standing;
- temporal standing;
- delegated standing;
- possible artificial standing.

### Context carrying capacity deepened

The concept is now presented as a multidimensional profile rather than one scalar and is explicitly related to:

- requisite variety;
- bounded rationality;
- cognitive load;
- information bottlenecks;
- privacy;
- protective omission.

### Temporal standing deepened

The paper now distinguishes temporal standing from:

- future-self continuity;
- intertemporal choice;
- personal identity;
- intergenerational justice;
- legal representation of future generations;
- fiduciary and succession mechanisms.

### Constitutional self boundary strengthened

The paper states directly that it does not discover internal plurality. It proposes a governance vocabulary built upon dialogical-self and parts-based traditions.

The non-clinical AI boundary is made explicit.

### Semantic-polytelometry architecture strengthened

A system must now preserve at least:

1. source attribution;
2. statement/inference separation;
3. participant correction;
4. uncertainty;
5. unresolved remainder;
6. model-role limits;
7. provenance;
8. consent boundaries;
9. resistance to automatic scalar collapse;
10. refusal, exit, or escalation.

### Primary sources added

The bibliography now includes work on:

- computational telic states and telic controllability;
- reversal theory;
- teleological centers of life;
- Parsons's telic system;
- future-self continuity;
- intergenerational standing.
