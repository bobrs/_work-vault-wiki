# Dependent Loops — Comparison and Boundary Matrix

Status: draft  
Content canon status: unset

| Concept | Decision | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Derived loop | Retain | Dependent on prior, lower, or outer conditions; not lesser or unreal |
| Constitutional substrate | Retain | Material, semantic, relational, institutional, telic, and capacity conditions |
| Telic compatibility | Retain | Compatible purpose, standing, authority, capacity, and repair—not cultural sameness |
| Telic incompatibility | Retain | Intended loop cannot be sustained without systematic coercion, drift, or substrate harm |
| Telic transplant failure | Retain | Imported form fails to establish the intended operative loop |
| Constitutional substrate mismatch | Retain | Required governance and standing conditions are absent or incompatible |
| Teleological graft rejection | Lineage/essay only | Biological metaphor; prohibit essentialist or xenophobic use |
| Hidden outer-loop recruitment | Retain | Local participation supplies an undisclosed outer purpose |
| Isomorphic mimicry | Adopt established term | Appearance of capability without operative capability |
| Premature load bearing | Adopt established term | Demands exceed the substrate and capacity available |
| Operative telos | Retain | Purpose inferred from what the loop restores under conflict; not hidden consciousness |
| Telic drift | Retain | Operative purpose changes without adequate witness or reconsent |
| Constitutional fork | Retain provisionally | Lineage-preserving separation of legitimately incompatible paths |
| Residual state | Retain | Data, models, habits, obligations, categories, and effects remaining after operation |
| Dissolution | Retain | Governed end of recruitment and authority; broader than shutdown |
| Aftercare loop | Adopt provisionally | Narrow successor for claims, repair, archives, or monitoring |

## Governing distinctions

```text
dependence ≠ failure
form ≠ loop
persistence ≠ health
survival ≠ legitimacy
adaptation ≠ drift
lock-in ≠ attachment
shutdown ≠ dissolution
dissolution ≠ erasure
release ≠ release from consequence
```
