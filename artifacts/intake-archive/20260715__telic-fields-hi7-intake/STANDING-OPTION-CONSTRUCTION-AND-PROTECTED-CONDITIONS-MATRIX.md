# Standing, Option Construction, and Protected Conditions Matrix

Status: candidate pre-production  
Content canon status: unset

| Governance question | Required record | Primary failure | Required response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who is affected? | Public-field assembly | Material consequence bearer absent | Add standing, represent, or pause |
| Why may a center participate? | Standing-admission record | Expertise or authority substituted for affected standing | Separate roles and scopes |
| Who speaks for an absent center? | Representation source and limits | Representation asserted without source | Admit conditionally or contest |
| Which routes exist? | Option-set witness | Agenda or option-set capture | Expand, combine, or reopen options |
| Who authored the route? | Option source and author class | Model or staff option presented as public demand | Correct authorship |
| Why was a route excluded? | Exclusion reason | Omission recorded as rejection | Restore as omitted or reopen |
| Which conditions resist tradeoff? | Protected-condition declaration | Boundary converted to weight | Apply noncompensatory gate |
| Who may override protection? | Override process | Rhetorical veto or silent override | Competent review |
| Which statement is a position? | Deliberative assertion class | Position, evidence, and prediction collapsed | Relabel and correct |
| Which summary governs? | Correctable active summary | Model summary treated as field | Participant review and correction |

## Standing distinctions

```text
standing ≠ factual correctness
expertise ≠ affected standing
representation ≠ ownership
admission ≠ veto
participation ≠ implementation authority
```

## Option-set minimum

## Governing principle

> The public field is shaped before any vote by who is admitted, which routes are imaginable, and which losses are permitted to enter tradeoff.
