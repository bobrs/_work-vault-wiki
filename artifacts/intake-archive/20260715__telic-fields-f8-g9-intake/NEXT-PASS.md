## Governing distinction

> **Polytely names the presence of many goals. Polytelometry names the disciplined effort to make their relations, authority, standing, consent, and consequences legible.**

## Governing boundary

The navigation system may propose routes.

It may not decide which center's field becomes sovereign merely because that field is easier to measure.
