# Succession and Inherited Obligation Matrix

Status: candidate pre-production  
Content canon status: unset

| Transfer class | Default treatment | Review question |
|---|---|---|
| Assets | Transfer only under documented title and restrictions | Which obligations attach to the asset? |
| Capabilities | Transfer as operational capacity | Which roles and credentials remain valid? |
| Authority | Revalidate by operation, scope, time, and source | May the successor describe, recommend, authorize, execute, adjudicate, or disclose? |
| Obligations | Transfer where continuity and governing terms support it | Can the obligation be fulfilled, narrowed, transferred, repaired, or released? |
| Records | Transfer under purpose and access restrictions | Who may see, use, correct, delete, or succeed the record? |
| Consent | Do not transfer merely because records transfer | Does the original scope permit successor use? |
| Public mandate | Revalidate through the governing constitutional process | Does the successor still represent the relevant public? |
| Unresolved harms | Preserve and assign responsibility | Who can repair, notify, compensate, or acknowledge? |
| Reputation | Transfer as historical association, not proof | Which claims remain accurate or contested? |
| Mission | Treat as a continuity claim | Does the substrate and authority still support it? |

## Successor claim rule

A successor claiming continuity for benefit should disclose:

```text
assets received
authority received
authority not received
obligations received
obligations disputed
restricted records received
unresolved harms received
review and release routes
```

## Dissolution boundary

Where no competent successor exists, dissolution must identify:

- residual assets;
- unresolved obligations;
- record custody;
- restricted data;
- active complaints;
- repair funds;
- deletion schedule;
- public notice;
- final witness.

## Governing principle

> A successor may inherit the duty to answer without inheriting every power once held by the predecessor.
