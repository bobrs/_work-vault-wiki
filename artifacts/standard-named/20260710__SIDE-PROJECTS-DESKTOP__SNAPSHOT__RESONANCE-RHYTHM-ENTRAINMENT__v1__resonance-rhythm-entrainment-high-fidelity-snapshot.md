## 1. Core Triad: Definitions That Now Behave Like Physics

### 1.1 Rhythm
**Rhythm** = temporal patterning of action within an agent or system.

Properties:
- repetition with variance
- predictable enough to stabilize
- flexible enough to adapt
- inherently *local*

Examples:
- breathing, gait
- work cadence
- speech timing
- micro‑rituals

**Key question**: *How does this agent move through time?*

---

### 1.2 Resonance
**Resonance** = selective amplification when rhythms share partial structure.

Properties:
- no force required
- does not require sameness
- pattern recognition + affect
- energy transfer without command

Resonance is the moment *meaning lights up*.

**Key question**: *What feels alive together?*

---

### 1.3 Entrainment
**Entrainment** = phase‑locking across agents or subsystems.

Properties:
- emergent, not fully controllable
- feedback tightens over time
- increases efficiency and risk simultaneously
- can enhance agency *or* dissolve it

**Key question**: *What are we now doing together — whether we intended to or not?*
