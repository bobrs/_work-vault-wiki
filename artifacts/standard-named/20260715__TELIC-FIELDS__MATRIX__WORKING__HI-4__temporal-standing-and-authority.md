# Temporal Standing and Authority Matrix

Status: candidate pre-production  
Content canon status: unset

| Temporal relation | What may persist | What must be revalidated | What must not be presumed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earlier self → present self | History, commitment, reliance, consequence | Scope, current context, authority, review conditions | Unchanged preference |
| Present self → later self | Consequence, option closure, obligation, material condition | Future-standing representation and reversibility | Future consent |
| Predecessor → successor | Assets, records, contracts, defined obligations | Authority by operation and scope | Unlimited inherited sovereignty |
| Earlier consent → new use | Historical witness and surviving obligations | Purpose, recipient, duration, version, renewal | Continuing consent |
| Earlier projection → current action | Historical explanation | Current validity and source recognition | Current direct evidence |
| Emergency authority → ordinary state | Historical necessity and consequence | Continuing need, expiry, competent review | Permanent emergency power |
| Commitment → changed circumstances | Reliance, completed work, residual obligation | Proportional continuation, narrowing, release | Captivity |
| Dissolved loop → residual state | Records, repair, unresolved obligation, provenance | Custody, deletion, successor responsibility | Continuing mission authority |

## Hard distinctions

```text
future standing ≠ future consent
continuity ≠ sameness
persistence ≠ legitimacy
history ≠ current authority
release ≠ erasure
succession ≠ unlimited transfer
rollback ≠ full repair
```

## Governing principle

> The present may act only with the authority it actually holds, while preserving the standing of those who will inherit what cannot later be corrected.
