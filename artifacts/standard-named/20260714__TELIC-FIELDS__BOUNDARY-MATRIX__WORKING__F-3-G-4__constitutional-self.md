# Constitutional Self — Boundary and Terminology Matrix

Status: draft  
Content canon status: unset

| Concept | Retain? | Boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| Constitutional self | Yes | Governance-oriented synthesis, not a diagnosis or discovery of internal plurality |
| Constitutional element | Cautiously | Ontology-neutral working representation; user language takes priority |
| Local internal standing | Yes | Right to representation in self-governance, not independent external sovereignty |
| Person-level sovereignty | Yes, hard invariant | No model, clinician, or institution may bypass the person through an inferred internal part |
| Executive configuration | Yes | Current control state, not the whole self |
| Executive authority | Yes | Scoped permission to act; distinct from behavioral dominance |
| Constitutional legitimacy | Provisionally | Normative governance test, not clinical measurement |
| Internal consent | Yes, defined narrowly | Adequate representation, boundaries, and review—not unanimity |
| Internal witness | Yes | Corrigibility, not surveillance or punishment |
| Internal telic field | Cautiously | Structured working map, not a measurable hidden field |
| Embodied standing | Yes | Body signals are evidence, not infallible truth |
| Core Self | User-selectable only | Not required by the framework |
| IFS part taxonomy | No default adoption | May be used by a person or clinician who chooses it |
| Structural-dissociation categories | Clinical use only | Not a default model of ordinary ambivalence |
| Recovered-memory interpretation | No | Experience must remain distinct from historical proof |
| AI parts therapy | No | AI may organize user-authored reflection but not diagnose or discover hidden parts |
| Atelic/paratelic protection | Yes | Rest, play, presence, privacy, and release may be valuable without instrumental purpose |

## Governing invariant

> **Internal representation may deepen person-level agency. It may never become a route around person-level sovereignty.**
