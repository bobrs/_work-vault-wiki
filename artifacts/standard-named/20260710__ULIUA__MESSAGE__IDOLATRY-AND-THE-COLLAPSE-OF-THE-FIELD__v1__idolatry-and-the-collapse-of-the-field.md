# Idolatry and the Collapse of the Field  
*A ULiUA Message*

Idolatry is not fundamentally about religion.  
It is about a category error.

It occurs when an **artifact**—a discrete, bounded, nameable thing—is treated as though it were the **attractor**—the living field of value, meaning, power, or love that the artifact only participates in.

The artifact is stable.  
The attractor is emergent.

Idolatry is the collapse of one into the other.

---

## The Structural Error

In healthy orientation:

- A car is enjoyed for mobility.
- Money is valued for optionality.
- A partner is cherished as a living participant in love.
- A ring is a symbol of commitment.

In idolatry:

- The car *is* freedom.
- Money *is* security.
- The partner *is* love.
- The ring *is* power.

The field collapses.

The artifact is promoted to base attractor.

And because artifacts are finite, this produces fear.

What can be lost must be defended.  
What must be defended becomes sacred.  
What becomes sacred cannot be questioned.

The loop tightens.

---

## Why Idolatry Feels So Powerful

Artifacts are graspable.

Attractors are not.

You can hold a ring.  
You cannot hold power.  
You can point to a person.  
You cannot point to love.

The human nervous system prefers handles.

Idolatry offers a handle.

It says: *“Here. This is it. Protect this.”*

And so the mind does.

Gollum does not love power in abstraction.  
He loves the ring.  
Because the ring can be clutched.

But the clutching is the trap.

---

## The Hidden Violence

Idolatry always contains a subtle violence.

When we treat an artifact as the attractor:

- We freeze what should be dynamic.
- We defend what should be fluid.
- We extract stability from what was meant to circulate.

Possession replaces participation.

The artifact is no longer a gateway into a living field.  
It becomes a sealed container of meaning.

And because no artifact can truly contain an attractor, anxiety follows.

This is why idolatry breeds obsession.

## The Inescapable Escape Plan

There is no strategy to defeat idolatry through destruction alone.

Destroy the ring, and another object will be crowned.  
Confiscate the idol, and a new one forms.

The error is structural.

The correction is ontological.

The escape plan is this:

**Accept every artifact as artifact.**

Not reject it.  
Not demonize it.  
Not renounce it.

Accept it.

(And here acceptance is not passive resignation, but a step on the path toward unconditional love—an orientation that refuses to collapse artifact into attractor.)

When an artifact is fully accepted as artifact:

- It regains replaceability.
- It regains contingency.
- It regains humility.

The attractor re-expands.

Love is no longer trapped in a person.  
Security is no longer trapped in money.  
Freedom is no longer trapped in a vehicle.

The field breathes again.
