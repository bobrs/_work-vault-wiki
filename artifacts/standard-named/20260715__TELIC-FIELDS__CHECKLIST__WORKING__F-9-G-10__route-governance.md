# Candidate Route-Governance Checklist

Status: research artifact  
Content canon status: unset

This checklist governs a route before selection or execution. It does not replace domain-specific legal, professional, democratic, or fiduciary process.

## 1. Orientation

- State the decision question.
- State the current decision authority.
- State the time horizon and reversibility.
- Identify the default consequence if no decision is made.

## 2. Standing admission

- Identify materially affected centers.
- Mark direct, delegated, absent, and uncertain representation.
- Identify future and outer-loop cost bearers.
- Record missing standing rather than assigning it a zero weight.

## 3. Telic classification

Separate:

```text
GOAL
VALUE
PREFERENCE
BOUNDARY
PROTECTED CONDITION
OBLIGATION
PREDICTION
CONSTRAINT
UNCERTAINTY
RELEASE CONDITION
```

Do not permit ordinary scalarization of protected conditions without explicit authority.

## 4. Field mapping

Classify relations as:

```text
SHARED
COMPATIBLE
CONDITIONAL
CONFLICTING
PROTECTED
UNRESOLVED
MISSING_STANDING
RELEASED
```

## 5. Route generation

Generate materially different routes, including where relevant:

- reversible trial;
- narrower scope;
- different sequence;
- compensation;
- local variation;
- fork;
- escalation;
- pause;
- release;
- no decision.

Attribute every machine-generated route.

## 6. Cost and consequence

For each route identify:

- beneficiary;
- direct cost bearer;
- risk bearer;
- future cost bearer;
- surveillance or privacy cost;
- repair burden;
- lock-in;
- residual state.

## 7. Analytical method

Declare any:

- Pareto analysis;
- MCDA method;
- weight meaning;
- outranking or veto threshold;
- scenario set;
- robustness rule;
- argumentation semantics;
- aggregation rule.

Record source, scope, authority, and uncertainty.

## 8. Governance gates

A route cannot enter execution until reviewed for:

- standing;
- consent;
- authority;
- capacity;
- privacy;
- stop conditions.

## 9. Decision and dissent

Record:

- selected route;
- decision rule;
- rationale;
- protected dissent;
- unresolved remainder;
- no-decision or refusal status;
- review and expiry.

## 10. Witness and learning

After action compare:

- projected and actual consequence;
- expected and actual cost bearers;
- represented and omitted standing;
- declared and operative teloi;
- predicted and actual reversibility.

Revise, compensate, fork, release, or dissolve where required.
