# Compatibility, Drift, Lock-In, and Capture Matrix

Status: candidate pre-production  
Content canon status: unset

| Condition | Evidence | Required response | Non-claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compatible transplant | Required substrate, authority, burden, correction, and exit are present | Proceed with review | Similar appearance is not enough |
| Compatible with adaptation | Most substrate exists but local changes are required | Adapt before expansion | Local difference is not defect |
| Substrate mismatch | Critical supporting condition is absent | Narrow, add substrate, or refuse transplant | Host is not biologically deficient |
| Mission drift | Declared and operative purposes diverge materially | Correct, reauthorize, fork, release, or dissolve | Change is not always wrong |
| Authority drift | Loop acts beyond the operation originally granted | Pause expansion and renew authority | Capability is not permission |
| Beneficiary drift | Value increasingly accrues to another center | Reassess standing and burden | Every indirect benefit is not drift |
| Burden drift | Maintenance or correction burden grows beyond grant | Redistribute, narrow, support, release, or dissolve | Output success does not erase burden |
| Moderate lock-in | Switching cost is visible and proportionate | Maintain portability and review | Some switching cost is unavoidable |
| High lock-in | Exit exists formally but is materially costly | Add transition, portability, and independent review | Dependence alone is not coercion |
| Capture | Support continues after effective refusal or withdrawal | Disable recruitment and initiate repair | Persistence is not consent |
| Releasing | Capture is being unwound | Track descendants and residual reliance | Release is not historical erasure |
| Released | Future recruitment ends | Preserve bounded witness and residual duties | Ending support does not erase consequence |

## Drift review

Material drift exists when one or more of the following changes:

```text
purpose
beneficiary
authority
scope
burden
risk
data use
protected condition
governance
exit
```

Rebranding does not constitute reauthorization.

## Metaphor boundary

“Teleological graft rejection” may remain as lineage language.

Technical records should prefer:

```text
telic incompatibility
telic transplant failure
constitutional substrate mismatch
```

No biological or clinical inference about participants follows from these terms.
