# Temporal Relations — Comparison and Boundary Matrix

Status: draft  
Content canon status: unset

| Neighboring concept | What it already explains | What temporal standing must add |
|---|---|---|
| Personal identity | Continuity, connectedness, bodily and psychological criteria | Governance without resolving metaphysical identity |
| Future-self continuity | Felt connection to later selves and future-oriented choice | Representation even when felt continuity is weak |
| Intertemporal choice | Tradeoffs among present and future outcomes | Protected conditions, authority, and irreversible loss |
| Commitment devices | Present self-binding against future preference change | Review, release, reliance, and anti-coercion safeguards |
| Advance directives | Earlier preferences under later incapacity | Simultaneous representation of earlier autonomy and later welfare |
| Fiduciary duty | Stewardship under entrusted power | Analogy for present governance, not literal legal classification |
| Intergenerational justice | Duties to future people and shared inheritance | General standing records, versioned authority, and repair paths |
| Legal standing for future generations | Jurisdiction-specific representation mechanisms | Broader moral and governance standing without false legal claims |
| Institutional succession | Transfer of authority, assets, liabilities, and duties | Purpose, consent, data obligations, and unresolved repair |
| Event sourcing | Append-only state-changing history and replay | Standing, consent, legitimacy, privacy, and release |
| Temporal databases | Valid time and transaction time | Authority, scope, consent, and current interpretive status |
| Temporal logic | Formal persistence, order, expiration, and eventuality | Constitutionally specified policies before formal enforcement |
| Versioned policy | Traceable changes in governing terms | Material-change legibility, refusal, exit, and historical action mapping |

## Governing distinctions

```text
continuity ≠ perfect sameness
history ≠ continuing authority
commitment ≠ permanent sovereignty
consent remembered ≠ consent renewed
record persistence ≠ legitimacy
future representation ≠ future consent
release ≠ historical erasure
version accuracy ≠ constitutional validity
```
