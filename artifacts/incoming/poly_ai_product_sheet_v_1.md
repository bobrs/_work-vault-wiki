# PolyAI  
## Constitutional Governance Engine for Regulated Decision Systems  

**PolyAI is a constraint-aware governance runtime that makes AI-assisted decisions auditable, reversible, and time-stable.**  

Built for highly regulated environments — including banking, accounting, and tribal gaming — PolyAI transforms AI from a productivity tool into a defensible governance substrate.

---

# Executive Summary

In regulated industries, the problem is no longer access to AI.

The problem is:

- Traceability  
- Authority control  
- Drift containment  
- Audit defensibility  
- Version correctness  
- Accountability  

PolyAI embeds governance directly into AI-assisted workflows.  
Every transformation is scoped, cited, time-anchored, attributable, and reversible.

Not a chatbot.  
Not a dashboard.  
A constitutional runtime.

---

# Core Capabilities

## 1. Time-Scoped Rule Intelligence
- Versioned GAAP / IRS / regulatory retrieval
- Effective-date filtering
- Supersession awareness
- Authority-tier filtering (Code vs Reg vs guidance vs secondary)

**Benefit:** Eliminates cross-year misapplication and unsupported interpretations.

---

## 2. Determination Legibility Engine
Each conclusion is separated into:
- Evidence (artifacts cited)
- Reasoning path
- Assumptions
- Risk exposure
- Confidence level

**Benefit:** Audit-ready explanations without reverse engineering.

---

## 3. Boundary Enforcement
- Jurisdiction scoping (state, federal, tribal, international)
- Policy layer separation
- Data domain containment
- Role-based scope control

**Benefit:** Prevents accidental cross-domain contamination and regulatory breaches.

---

## 4. Authority Gradient Enforcement
PolyAI distinguishes:
- Binding law
- Interpretive guidance
- Industry commentary
- Internal policy

**Benefit:** Stops secondary sources from being mistaken as authoritative.

---

## 5. Constitutional Invariant Layer
Optional enterprise-defined invariants such as:
- Preserve reversibility
- No silent compression of dissent
- Maintain consent continuity
- Require citation for determinations

**Benefit:** Governance becomes architectural, not procedural.

---

## 6. Identity & Accountability Anchoring
- Determinations attributable to agent, role, and version
- “Knowledge at time of decision” preserved
- Model/version fingerprinting

**Benefit:** Eliminates anonymous cognition at scale.

---

## 7. Risk & Sensitivity Exposure
Each output can declare:
- What would change the conclusion
- Missing inputs
- Regulatory gray zones
- Assumption dependencies

**Benefit:** Reduces regulatory surprise and post-hoc liability.
