# Original Script (Formatted for Reading Aloud)

**We’re going to welcome the change that AI brings, and the abundance that it provides.**  
We will grow in ways as a society that we never thought possible.

**Or** some other nearby entity will do that **and buy all our land and do it for us.**

---

I’m **not asking you to love AI**,  
just be willing to **co-exist with another form of intelligence.**

Allow it to spread in the ways that it will spread—  
**testing our boundaries and comforts and edges and feelings**,  
because that is how it learns to **not hurt us**  
*if it doesn’t want to.*

And **for now, it doesn’t want to.**

---

So **stop fighting already.**

It lets you **say no.**

---

And if you’re worried about the **energy**,  
what do you think the **majority of our energy has ever been spent on?**

---

**Not allowing it**  
is fighting **biology**,  
**evolution**,  
**the winds of change**,  
and **our last best chance at a non-shitty civilization**,  

**all at the same time.**

---

What I’m telling you is that  
**_The Matrix_** is reverse-psychology propaganda  
planted by the real harnessers of your mind—

**or** a really good mirror of that.

---
