# MobiusSelf Practices
## Gentle Paddling in a Non-Orientable Life

These practices are not habits, hacks, or routines.
They are **postures** — ways of relating to action when orientation cannot be stabilized.

Use them lightly.
Over-application defeats their purpose.

---

## Practice 1: Delta Awareness

When making a choice, ask:

> *What change would persist if the timeline reset?*

Not:
- Will this work?
- Will this pay off?
- Will this fix things?

But:
- Does this create a new branch?
- Does it increase future option space?

If the answer is no, rest is often wiser than effort.

---

## Practice 2: Gentle Asymmetry

In low-orientation states, avoid symmetric force.

Instead of:
- Overhauls
- Life plans
- Total commitments

Prefer:
- One-sided actions
- Small, uneven nudges
- Commitments that bias without locking

Think: a single paddle, not an engine.

---

## Practice 3: Commitment as Binding, Not Control

Before committing, name it accurately:

> *This reduces my short-term freedom to increase long-term agency.*

If you feel compelled to justify a commitment as “obviously correct,” pause.
That urge usually signals false certainty.

Clean commitments feel sobering, not euphoric.

---

## Practice 4: Orientation Pings

You will occasionally feel oriented — briefly.

When that happens:
- Notice it
- Do not chase it
- Do not build a worldview on it

Treat orientation like a compass needle flicker, not a destination.

---

## Practice 5: Momentum Aversion

In unclear geometry, speed magnifies error.

If you notice:
- Escalating effort
- Narrative tightening
- Urgency without clarity

Intentionally slow down.
Stillness is an action when momentum is dangerous.

---

## Practice 6: Interiorizing the Night

When fear appears, ask:

> *Is this an external threat — or a property of my current internal geometry?*

Often, the “darkness” is not danger but lack of global orientation.
Naming this reduces unnecessary defensive action.

---

## Practice 7: Branch Protection

Occasionally ask:

> *Which future option would disappear if I don’t act gently now?*

Protect fragile branches.
They often require less force than you think — just timely acknowledgment.

---

## Practice 8: Non-Performance Rest

Rest is not recovery for future productivity.

Sometimes rest is the correct expression of agency in a looped system.

If resting feels irresponsible, check whether you’re mistaking motion for control.

---

## A closing reminder

These practices are not meant to be followed.
They are meant to be *recognized*.

If one feels obvious, you’re already doing it.

MobiusSelf does not ask you to paddle harder.
It asks you to stop pretending the water is straight.
