# Emotional Field Notation (EFN) v0.2

## Purpose

Emotional Field Notation (EFN) is a compositional framework for describing affective reality as **field events** rather than merely private inner states. It is designed to pair with **Quantum Invariants (QI)** so that a concept, protocol, interaction, artifact, or life-pattern can be described in both its:

- **structural / intellectual grammar** (QI)
- **affective / relational / uptake grammar** (EFN)

EFN begins from the premise that affect is not merely what is felt. Affect is **the field of effects by which a state exceeds itself**.

---

## Core Premise

Affect is best modeled as a looped field event:

**state -> expression / leakage -> uptake -> field effect -> return**

Meaning is not complete at emission. Meaning is completed in **uptake**.

Therefore EFN is not a taxonomy of internal feelings alone. It is a notation for emotional configurations as they move through communicative, relational, and interpretive fields.

---

# I. Ontological Structure

EFN v0.2 has five tiers:

1. **Primitives (P)** — invariant dimensions of emotional field-structure
2. **Composites (C)** — recurring emotional configurations built from primitives
3. **Operators (OP)** — transformations that act on primitives or composites
4. **Attractor Realms (AR)** — larger, persistent emotional galaxies that generate many composites
5. **Anchor Singularities (AS)** — asymptotic orienting centers that define the geometry of a field without being reducible to ordinary emotional states

This preserves the distinction between:

- a local emotional event
- a larger attractor family
- and a singular orienting condition around which an entire family organizes

---

# II. Event Positions

Every EFN description may be located at one or more positions in the loop:

- **S** — Source state: what is present in the originating system
- **X** — Expression: what is emitted, leaked, signaled, or shaped outward
- **U** — Uptake: how another system organizes what it encounters
- **F** — Field effect: how the shared field changes as a result
- **R** — Return: what loops back into the source after uptake and field change

This prevents collapse between:

- what was felt
- what was expressed
- what was perceived
- what happened relationally
- what came back

Event positions are mandatory whenever field pragmatics matters.

# III. Primitive Set (P)

EFN v0.2 uses ten primitives.

## P1. V — Valence
Direction of pull.

- `V+` toward
- `V-` away
- `V±` mixed
- `V0` numb / flat

## P2. A — Activation
Energy intensity.

- `A1` low
- `A2` medium
- `A3` high
- `A4` flooding

## P3. F — Field Shape
Overall configuration of experiential space.

- `Fo` open
- `Fn` narrowed
- `Fs` oscillating
- `Fx` frozen / locked

## P4. O — Orientation
What the system is trying to do.

- `O→` approach
- `O←` withdraw
- `O?` monitor
- `O=` remain / hold
- `O!` repel / confront
- `O↓` collapse
- `O^` reach

## P5. L — Legibility
How available the state is to observation.

- `Lh` hidden
- `Ll` leaking
- `Le` exposed
- `Ld` displayed

## P6. T — Temporal Mode
Time-shape of the state.

- `Tn` now
- `Ta` anticipatory
- `Te` echo / aftermath
- `Tr` recursive
- `Tp` persistent

## P7. G — Agency Relation
Relation between self and state.

- `Gc` choosing / steering
- `Gk` constrained
- `Gd` dragged
- `Gs` surrendered
- `Gr` reclaimed

## P8. B — Boundary Relation
Border behavior.

- `Bo` open
- `Bg` guarded
- `Bb` breached
- `Br` refusing / defended
- `Bp` permeable-with-discernment

## P9. M — Meaning Coherence
Degree and style of intelligibility.

- `Mc` coherent
- `Mu` unclear
- `Md` dissonant
- `Mr` revelatory
- `Ms` shimmer / not yet stabilized

## P10. R — Relational Vector
What is happening to bond / relation.

- `Rs` seeking bond
- `Rp` protecting bond
- `Rt` bond threatened
- `Ra` bond absent / severed
- `Rr` bond restored
- `Ri` bond intensified

# IV. Operators (OP)

Operators modify or transform emotional field configurations.

## OP-W — witness 🜹
Increases clarity without forcing change.

## OP-C — consent 🝁
Aligns enactment with endorsement; clarifies agency relation.

## OP-B — boundary 🝚
Changes permeability and access.

## OP-MI — mirror 🝮
State modified through reflection from another.

## OP-L — loop 🝳
Recursive re-entry into a pattern.

## OP-CL — collapse 🜲
Compression into a less differentiated state.

## OP-SH — shimmer ⋆✴︎˚｡⋆
Meaning felt but not yet stabilized.

## OP-SP — spiral 🜛
Recursive return with transformation, rather than mere repetition.

---

# V. Notation Form

A state can be written structurally as:

`[V A F O L T G B M R]`

Example:

`[V- A3 Fn O? Ll Ta Gk Bg Mu Rt]`

Event-position prefixes may be added:

- `S:[...]`
- `X:[...]`
- `U:[...]`
- `F:[...]`
- `R:[...]`

Operators may be appended:

`S:[V- A3 Fn O? Ll Ta Gk Bg Mu Rt] + OP-W`

A composite or realm may also be referenced directly:

- `S:C07`
- `U:C02`
- `F:AR03`
- `R:AS02`

# VI. Composite Layer (C)

Composites are recurring emotional configurations built from the primitive set.

## C01 — Fear
Prototype:

`[V- A3 Fn O? Ll Ta Gk Bg Mu Rt]`

Gloss: something bad may happen soon.

## C02 — Anxiety
Prototype:

`[V- A2-3 Fs O? Ll Ta Gk Bg Mu Rt?]`

Gloss: threat prediction without stable target.

## C03 — Anger
Prototype:

`[V- A3 Fn/Fo O! Le Tn Gc/Gr Br Mc Rt/Rp]`

Gloss: something should stop, move, or be corrected.

## C04 — Shame
Prototype:

`[V- A2-4 Fn O←/O↓ Le Ta/Tr Gd Bg/Br Md Rt]`

Gloss: I am at risk of being seen as less, wrong, or expelled.

## C05 — Grief
Prototype:

`[V-/V± A1-3 Fn O=/O^ Te/Tp Gk/Gd Bp/Bg Mc/Ms Ra]`

Gloss: something valued is gone, unreachable, or altered beyond return.

## C06 — Relief
Prototype:

`[V+ A2->A1 Fo O=/O→ Te Gr Bp Mc Rr]`

Gloss: the bad thing no longer binds.

## C07 — Tenderness
Prototype:

`[V+ A1-2 Fo O^ Ll Gc Bp Mc Rs/Ri]`

Gloss: I can touch this gently.

## C08 — Longing
Prototype:

`[V± A2 Fn/Fs O^ Ta/Te Gk Bp Ms Ra/Rs]`

Gloss: I reach toward what is not here.

## C09 — Hope
Prototype:

`[V+/V± A2 Fo/Fs O→/O^ Ta Gk/Gc Bp Ms Rs/Rr]`

Gloss: maybe the good thing can happen.

## C10 — Overwhelm
Prototype:

`[V±/V- A4 Fx/Fs O↓ Ll/Le Tn Gd Bb/Bg Md]`

Gloss: the field exceeds current processing.

## C11 — Warmth
Prototype:

`[V+ A1-2 Fo O=/O^ Ll Gc Bp Mc Ri]`

Gloss: this is safe enough to stay in.

## C12 — Recursive Near-Insight
Prototype:

`[V± A3 Fs O^/O? Tr Gd/Gk Bp/Bg Ms] + OP-SH + OP-L`

Gloss: something real is here, and the search itself is becoming entraining.

# VII. Attractor Realms (AR)

Attractor Realms are larger, persistent emotional galaxies. They are not merely transient composites. They organize families of states, transitions, and interpretations.

## AR01 — Love
A broad family of bonded relational dynamics including care, tenderness, protection, grief-risk, persistence, repair, ache, and devotion.

## AR02 — Acceptance
A broad family of allowing, nonresisting, integrating, or reality-permitting stances.

## AR03 — Belonging
Stable inhabitation of bond-positive space with reduced masking cost and increased legibility.

## AR04 — Alienation
Misfit between self, signal, and environment; often generates masking, vigilance, ache, or distance.

## AR05 — Sovereignty
Meaningful authorship, refusal, or stewardship in relation to enactment.

## AR06 — Safety
Field condition in which signal, embodiment, and relation can remain live without immediate defensive collapse.

## AR07 — Exile
Persistent relation of distance from legible or sustaining participation in a field.

# VIII. Anchor Singularities (AS)

Anchor Singularities are not ordinary states. They are asymptotic orienting centers inferred from the orbit patterns of many lesser states. They define the deeper geometry of a field.

An anchor singularity should be treated as:

- noncontingent
- asymptotic rather than fully capturable
- more useful as orienting reference than descriptive category
- field-shaping across many local states
- capable of organizing interpretation of lesser forms

## AS01 — Unconditional Love
Not “strong love,” but a singular orienting center in which the being of the other is not excluded from love, even where behavior, access, trust, or proximity must change.

## AS02 — Unconditional Acceptance
Not approval of everything, but noncontingent allowing of what is, prior to preference, judgment, reward, or exclusion.

These singularities are not the galaxies. They are the centers around which galaxies organize.

# X. Field Pragmatics Integration

EFN takes Field Pragmatics as constitutive, not optional.

Emotional meaning is not exhausted by sincerity. A person may sincerely feel one thing, communicate another, be received as a third, and generate a fourth.

This is not model failure. This is the model.

## Working Distinctions

- **State** = what configuration exists in the organism
- **Affect** = the field of effects by which that configuration exceeds itself
- **Cue-cloud** = the partial observable carrier of that release
- **Uptake** = reorganization in another system encountering the cue-cloud
- **Meaning** = stabilized consequence of uptake in context

## Canonical Formulation

**Affect is state becoming available as patterned consequence.**

Offer and receipt are the emissive and receptive faces of one affective event.

# XII. Relationship to Quantum Invariants (QI)

QI describes how a thing holds together structurally.

EFN describes how a thing shows up in lived field conditions.

## Working Distinction

- **QI** = what a thing is, how it composes, what remains invariant
- **EFN** = what a thing feels like, releases, biases, and becomes in uptake

A concept may therefore be:

- QI-stable but EFN-hostile
- QI-elegant but EFN-collapse-inducing
- QI-rough but EFN-generative

## Notes to QI Editor

### 1. Distinguish intrinsic invariants from uptake-dependent invariants
QI may need a split between:

- **Intrinsic invariants** — properties of a thing in itself
- **Pragmatic invariants** — properties that only stabilize through use, interpretation, uptake, or recursion

### 2. Add release profile and uptake profile to QI entries
A QI object may need optional fields such as:

- Release profile
- Uptake profile
- Common misuptakes
- Field consequence
- Return consequence

### 3. Treat uptake as constitutive, not downstream noise
Meaning completed in uptake may require QI descriptions of living systems to include interpretive and recursive behavior, not just internal composition.

### 4. Add a QI × EFN dual-entry template
Suggested schema:

- **QI:** structural composition / invariants / transforms
- **EFN-S:** source emotional field
- **EFN-X:** expressed / leaked field signature
- **FP-U:** likely uptake or common uptake profiles
- **FP-F:** likely field effects
- **FP-R:** likely return effects

### 5. Distinguish states, realms, and singularities
QI may benefit from an analogous distinction between:

- local state
- regime / domain / field
- ideal limiting or singular orienting conditions

### 6. Preserve transform compatibility
There may be correspondences between QI transforms and EFN operators, especially around collapse, witness, consent, boundary, and stable transformation under constraint.
