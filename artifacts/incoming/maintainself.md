# MaintainSelf

**Loop hygiene:** a gentle way to let what’s no longer needed rest — without self-surveillance.

---

## What this is

MaintainSelf is not self-improvement. It’s maintenance: reducing background interference so you can live and steer without dragging stale loops forward.

> **What’s already happening is enough.**  
> **What’s no longer needed can rest.**

If this ever feels like something you’re doing “correctly” — or checking on — that’s your cue to soften or stop. Stopping is part of the design.

---

## When to use it

- When you feel the “same puzzle, new costume” vibe.
- When you notice looping, urgency, or internal debate.
- When you’re tired of trying to fix yourself.
- Before you attempt a big “programming” change.

*Not a replacement for medical or mental health care. If you’re in crisis, seek professional support.*

---

## The 2-minute Loop Hygiene practice

1. **Pause.** One breath. No goal.
2. **Permission.** Say (silently): *“What’s already happening is enough.”*
3. **Rest.** Say: *“What’s no longer needed can rest.”*
4. **No monitoring.** Don’t look for results. Don’t scan for what changed.
5. **Return.** Put attention on one sensation (breath, weight, sound) and continue your day.

If a loop grabs you mid-day, use the micro-version:  
`Enough. Rest. Nothing to decide right now.`

---

## Anti-loop clause (non-negotiable)

MaintainSelf must not become a new compulsion. Use this rule:

> **If this ever feels like something you’re doing, you can stop.**

Anything useful will return on its own. Anything that isn’t, doesn’t need to be carried forward.
