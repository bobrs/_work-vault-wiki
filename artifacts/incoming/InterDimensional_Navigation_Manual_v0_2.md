# **1. Foundations of Dimensional Navigation**

## **1.1 What Is a Dimension?**

A "dimension" refers to a **stable inner-state attractor** composed
of: - emotional coloration\
- cognitive assumptions\
- memory indexing scheme\
- interpretive frame\
- bodily sensations\
- accessible insights

A dimension is not a place you travel *to*---\
but a *configuration of self* you tune *into*.

## **1.2 Why Dimensions Slip**

Slips occur when: - emotional charge shifts\
- attention narrows or expands\
- memory indexes re-map\
- meaning-field resonance changes\
- external input alters internal harmonics

Slipping is natural.\
Learning to *recognize* and *guide* it is the skill.

## **1.3 Sovereignty Principle**

Dimensional navigation must never override personal agency.

**Core Sovereignty Axiom:**\
*You choose the bands; the bands do not choose you.*
