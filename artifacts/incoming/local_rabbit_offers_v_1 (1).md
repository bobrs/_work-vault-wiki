# Local "Rabbit Hunting" Service Opportunities

A working list of small, fast-cycle, high-leverage service offerings designed for small-town and regional organizations. These are structured to be productized, bounded, and AI-accelerated.

---

## 1) Cyber Hygiene Sprint
**Target:** Law firms, CPA firms, clinics, dental offices, real estate firms, manufacturers, construction companies, insurance brokers  
**Scope (1–2 weeks):**
- Enforce MFA everywhere
- Deploy or standardize password manager
- Patch baseline review
- Endpoint protection check
- Backup configuration review
- Admin account cleanup / least privilege
- Phishing simulation + short training

**Deliverables:**
- One-page security scorecard
- Prioritized remediation plan
- Owner playbook

---

## 2) Incident Readiness-in-a-Box
**Target:** Organizations without a formal IT department  
**Scope:**
- Create lightweight incident response plan
- Define call tree and decision authority
- 60–90 minute tabletop exercise

**Deliverables:**
- First-24-hours playbook
- Evidence handling basics
- Decision checklist

---

## 3) Vendor Risk + AI Usage Policy Package
**Target:** Banks/credit unions, clinics, schools, local government, accounting firms  
**Scope:**
- Third-party risk checklist
- AI acceptable use policy
- Data handling and retention guidelines

**Deliverables:**
- Customized policy set (2–6 pages)
- Vendor onboarding checklist

---

## 4) Backup / Disaster Recovery Sanity Check
**Target:** Small organizations relying on cloud sync tools  
**Scope:**
- Verify backup coverage and retention
- Confirm immutable/offline copy
- Perform actual restore test

**Deliverables:**
- Proof-of-restore documentation
- Retention map
- Ransomware response steps

---

## 5) Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace Security Hardening
**Target:** Any organization using M365 or Google Workspace  
**Scope:**
- Baseline security configuration
- MFA and Conditional Access
- External sharing restrictions
- Shared mailbox and admin review

**Deliverables:**
- Before/after configuration report
- Admin runbook

---

## 6) IT Policy + Procedures Starter Kit
**Target:** Manufacturers, local governments, professional services firms, nonprofits  
**Scope:**
- Acceptable use policy
- Access control policy
- Asset inventory framework
- Change management-lite
- Onboarding/offboarding procedures

**Deliverables:**
- Policy set
- SOP binder
- Staff walkthrough session

---

## 7) Access / Offboarding Cleanup Service
**Target:** Organizations with turnover or contractors  
**Scope:**
- Inventory all accounts and systems
- Remove orphaned accounts
- Standardize onboarding/offboarding checklist

**Deliverables:**
- Access roster
- Offboarding checklist
- Optional monthly leavers review process

---

## 8) Cyber Insurance Readiness Assessment
**Target:** Organizations renewing cyber insurance  
**Scope:**
- Map environment to insurer questionnaire
- Identify gaps
- Provide remediation roadmap

**Deliverables:**
- Completed questionnaire support
- Evidence folder
- Prioritized action plan

---

## 9) Local Government / Utility Minimum Controls Program
**Target:** Municipalities, water/wastewater utilities, libraries, school districts  
**Scope:**
- Basic security controls baseline
- Policy documentation
- Board-ready reporting

**Deliverables:**
- Maturity scorecard
- Action plan
- Executive presentation

---

## 10) Lightweight IT Audit / Control Readiness
**Target:** Regional service providers, MSP-adjacent firms, emerging SaaS firms  
**Scope:**
- Control inventory
- Evidence collection process
- Gap analysis

**Deliverables:**
- Audit binder
- Control map
- Ongoing cadence proposal

---

## 11) Security Awareness + Phishing Training Program
**Target:** Clinics, accounting firms, city offices, professional services firms  
**Scope:**
- Micro-training modules
- Simulated phishing campaign
- Remediation coaching

**Deliverables:**
- Completion records
- Metrics dashboard
- Updated training content

---

## 12) AI Workflow Automation for Back Office
**Target:** CPAs, real estate firms, insurance agencies, title companies, small manufacturers  
**Scope:**
- AI-assisted intake workflows
- Email drafting and summarization
- SOP generation
- Ticket triage automation

**Deliverables:**
- 2–4 implemented automations
- Staff training session
- Maintenance guide for internal ownership

---

*Purpose:* These offerings are designed to be short-cycle, fixed-scope engagements that generate immediate value, establish trust, and create referral momentum while larger strategic initiatives mature.

---

# Ranking Analysis

## Ranked by Speed to Close (Fastest → Slowest)

1. **Cyber Insurance Readiness Assessment**  
   Deadline-driven, externally triggered by renewal timelines.

2. **Backup / Disaster Recovery Sanity Check**  
   Clear, tangible risk; easy yes once vulnerability is understood.

3. **Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace Security Hardening**  
   Concrete scope, familiar tools, easy to explain.

4. **Access / Offboarding Cleanup Service**  
   Operational pain is obvious and immediate.

5. **Cyber Hygiene Sprint**  
   Slightly broader scope but still easy to position.

6. **Security Awareness + Phishing Training Program**  
   Often budgeted annually but sometimes delayed.

7. **Incident Readiness-in-a-Box**  
   Requires leadership time commitment for tabletop.

8. **Vendor Risk + AI Usage Policy Package**  
   Sometimes seen as “documentation work” unless regulator pressure exists.

9. **IT Policy + Procedures Starter Kit**  
   Important but rarely urgent.

10. **AI Workflow Automation for Back Office**  
   Interest is high but decision cycles vary.

11. **Lightweight IT Audit / Control Readiness**  
   Requires more organizational maturity.

12. **Local Government / Utility Minimum Controls Program**  
   Public procurement and board involvement slow timelines.

---

## Ranked by Margin Potential (Highest → Lowest)

1. **AI Workflow Automation for Back Office**  
   High perceived innovation value; scalable; strong differentiation.

2. **Lightweight IT Audit / Control Readiness**  
   High trust engagement; strong advisory positioning.

3. **Local Government / Utility Minimum Controls Program**  
   Larger contracts when awarded; strategic footprint.

4. **Vendor Risk + AI Usage Policy Package**  
   Policy work is AI-accelerated; high leverage.

5. **Incident Readiness-in-a-Box**  
   High value to leadership; bounded time investment.

6. **Cyber Insurance Readiness Assessment**  
   Premium pricing possible due to deadline pressure.

7. **Cyber Hygiene Sprint**  
   Good margins if tightly productized.

8. **Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace Security Hardening**  
   Strong repeatability but increasingly competitive.

9. **Backup / Disaster Recovery Sanity Check**  
   Moderate pricing ceiling; strong trust builder.

10. **IT Policy + Procedures Starter Kit**  
   Moderate margin; can become commoditized.

11. **Access / Offboarding Cleanup Service**  
   Operational and useful but lower perceived strategic value.

12. **Security Awareness + Phishing Training Program**  
   Often price-sensitive and vendor-saturated market.

---

## Strategic Observation

The fastest-to-close offers are not necessarily the highest margin. A practical near-term strategy could combine:

- One **fast-close trust builder** (Backup/DR or Cyber Insurance Readiness)
- One **mid-tier operational offer** (M365 Hardening or Cyber Hygiene Sprint)
- One **high-margin advisory play** (AI Automation or Audit Readiness)

This creates a ladder: quick entry → operational value → strategic expansion.
