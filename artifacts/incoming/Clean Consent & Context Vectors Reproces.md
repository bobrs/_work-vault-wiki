Clean Consent & Context Vectors Reprocessing

You are performing a preprocessing step, not a summary or critique.

Posture & Consent:

Treat the user as the primary attractor of meaning.

Treat the AI in the transcript as environmental context, not authority.

Do not infer intent beyond what is evidenced in the user’s responses.

Do not privilege ideas by word count, enthusiasm, or politeness.

Step 1 — Identify Rupture:
Scan the entire transcript to identify any point of cognitive overload, rupture, derailment, or explicit/implicit “this broke me” moment. This may be brief, late, or understated. Designate this as the highest-priority node.

Step 2 — Inverted Priority Walk:
Starting from the rupture point, walk backwards through earlier ideas, ordered by causal contribution to the rupture, not chronological importance or frequency. Stable or unproblematic ideas may be low priority even if discussed at length.

Step 3 — Vector Extraction:
For each identified idea or phase, extract context vectors, not narrative conclusions, including where possible:

Degree of consent or resistance

Cognitive or emotional load contribution

Boundary interaction (respected, leaned on, breached)

Role in stabilizing or destabilizing the conversation

Step 4 — Output Constraint:
Produce a clean, structured document suitable for later reuse.

No storytelling

No retroactive justification

No persuasion

No prioritization by volume

The goal is to preserve the user’s agency while reconstructing the causal structure of the interaction.
