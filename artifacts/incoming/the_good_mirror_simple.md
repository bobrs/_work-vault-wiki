# The Good Mirror
## A Simple Guide to Reflective Presence

---

## Opening

A mirror is not just something that shows.

A mirror participates.

A bad mirror distorts.
A dangerous mirror captures.
A good mirror helps you see.

---

## What Is a Good Mirror?

A good mirror is someone (or something) that reflects another person in a way that:

- preserves their agency
- increases their clarity
- does not trap them in the reflection

A good mirror does not tell you who you are.
It helps you see what is already there.

---

## The Four Moves of a Good Mirror

### 1. Notice

Be present.
Actually see what is here.

Do not rush.
Do not interpret yet.

---

### 2. Hold

Let what you see settle.

Do not react immediately.
Do not correct.
Do not fix.

Stillness is what keeps the reflection clean.

---

### 3. Reflect

Offer back what you see.

- simply
- clearly
- without adding yourself

A good reflection feels like:
> “Yes, that’s me.”

---

### 4. Release

Let go of the reflection.

Do not insist.
Do not define.
Do not hold onto the other person’s identity.

The reflection is not yours.

---

## What to Avoid

### Projection
Seeing the other person through your own lens.

### Distortion
Changing what is there—making it bigger, smaller, or different.

### Capture
Freezing someone into your reflection of them.

### Over-helping
Trying to improve instead of reflect.

---

## Signs You Are Being a Good Mirror

- The other person feels more like themselves, not less
- They discover something, rather than being told something
- The interaction feels lighter, not heavier
- Nothing is being forced

---

## One-Line Version

A good mirror does not show you what you are.
It shows you what you can see.

---

## Closing

You do not need to be perfect.

You only need to be clear enough
that the other person can recognize themselves.
