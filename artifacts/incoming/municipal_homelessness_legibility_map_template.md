> **Purpose:** Replace vague claims ("we don’t have a problem" / "cost is 0") with a **loop-closed accounting** of (1) *what happens to people*, (2) *what it costs locally*, and (3) *what costs are exported.*

> **Use:** Print this as a checklist. If a municipality cannot answer these fields, the missing answers indicate **where details are being buried.**

## H) Legibility tests (quick red flags)
Check any that apply:
- [ ] Budget shows “$0 for housing homeless” but no displacement/transport line items.
- [ ] City counts “referrals” as outcomes; no “placements achieved” metric.
- [ ] Resources page lists providers without capacity/wait-time data.
- [ ] Sweeps occur without tracking seizures/returns and downstream harms.
- [ ] No destination reporting for transports (even aggregated).
- [ ] “Public health / hygiene” language used without proportionality/due-process safeguards.

## Notes
- **Privacy:** destination + repeat metrics can be aggregated/anonymized; “privacy” should not block basic flow accounting.
- **Language hygiene:** replace “cleanup” with “displacement + remediation” in analysis. The moral choice often hides in euphemisms.
- **Framing:** the goal is not blame; it is **loop closure**: policy → movement → cost → outcomes → responsibility.
