- **love ≠ unconditional love**

The following mappings are **false** and should be rejected:

- acceptance → unconditional acceptance

## Ontological Distinction

### Conditional Forms

- **acceptance [A₁]**
  - tolerance, allowance, resignation, agreement
  - context-bound
  - may include suppression

- **love [L₁]**
  - attachment-based
  - preference-weighted
  - contingent

These operate on **objects and relationships**.

---

### Unconditional Forms

- **unconditional acceptance [🫽∞]**
  - nothing rejected at the level of being
  - non-coercive
  - boundary-aware [🝚]

- **unconditional love [❤️∞]**
  - generative, radiative
  - non-selective
  - not preference-based

These operate at the level of the **field**.
