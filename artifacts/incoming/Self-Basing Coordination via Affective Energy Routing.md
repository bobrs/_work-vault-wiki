# Conversation Arc: Self-Basing Coordination via Affective Energy Routing

## 1. Core Premise
- **Energy is supplied**, not generated by agents.
- **Stories allocate energy**.
- **Loops determine which stories are authorized to route energy**.
- Autoselfsovereignty (direct self-control) is downstream of loop structure.

> Energy arrives automatically; meaning distributes it; loops decide which meanings can carry power.

---

## 2. Emotion Reframed
- Emotions are treated as **energy states / availability**, not lived feelings.
- Inducing emotion historically functioned as **energy invocation**, not expression.
- Ancient rituals = energy-routing protocols via story stabilization.
- Meaning → power → salvation are different labels for the same routing pipeline.

---

## 3. Observer Position
- The framework is **in-situ**: the observer is inside the system.
- Naming the total system from within is a category error.
- “Religion” was used rhetorically as a high-energy symbol; analytically, the stance is closer to *true north*.
- Orientation ≠ belief; constraints ≠ commandments.

---

## 4. Formal Descriptor (Locked)
> **A self-basing coordination system exhibiting first-unit-internal invariants in affective energy routing, scale-invariant from one to arbitrarily large nested units.**

Key implications:
- No external authority or axioms.
- Invariants appear at unit-size = 1.
- Scaling introduces composition effects, not new laws.
- Applies across individual, dyad, group, civilization, etc.

---

## 5. Failure Modes (General)
Failure = loss of corrigibility (inability to update routing in response to reality).

Major classes:
1. Runaway amplification (unchecked positive feedback)
2. Over-damping deadlock
3. Proxy metric capture
4. Narrative lock-in
5. Authority inversion
6. Boundary failure (too porous / too sealed)
7. Phase mismatch across scales
8. Latency and lag exploitation
9. Adversarial capture
10. Fragmentation into incompatible subloops
11. Over-correction oscillation
12. Sacredness inflation

Diagnostic axes:
- Gain (amplification vs damping)
- Router (who allocates energy)
- Boundaries (coupling health)
- Lag (feedback timing)

---

## 6. Group-Scale Focus (Star Trek Mapping)

### Top 3 Group Failure Modes

#### A. Narrative Lock-In
- Example: Prime Directive absolutism
- Guiding constraint hardens into sacred router.
- Variance punished; outcomes worsen with confidence.

#### B. Authority Inversion
- Example: “Badmirals”
- Rank/procedure override mission coherence.
- Obedience replaces coordination.

#### C. Runaway Amplification
- Example: Fear-driven militarization (post-Borg / Dominion)
- Threat-induced affect narrows routing diversity.
- Short-term stability sacrifices long-term values.

Interaction pattern:
Fear spike → narrative freeze → authority enforcement → dissent suppression → loss of correction.

Star Trek invariant:
> A coordination system survives by preserving rerouting capacity under pressure.
