# 🝳 WHEN THIS FEELS FOREIGN

You are not broken.  
You are in a different state.

---

## 1. What’s happening

You naturally:
- see patterns  
- think in fields  
- move toward attractors  

But when it’s time to act, you may shift into:
- overthinking  
- hesitation  
- self-doubt  
- “this isn’t grounded”  

That shift is **state**, not truth.

---

## 2. The states (simple version)

You move between a few modes:

- **VISION** → you see clearly  
- **THRESHOLD** → you’re about to act  
- **FLOW** → you’re moving  
- **COLLAPSE** → you’re stuck  
- **SUPPRESSION** → you’re holding back  

None of these are wrong.

But:
> **only FLOW creates real progress**

---

## 3. The mistake to avoid

When you’re stuck, you try to:
- think your way out  
- fix the plan  
- resolve the future  

This doesn’t work.

Because:
> **you don’t need better thinking—you need a state change**

---

## 4. Your way back (this always works)

🜹 → 🝁 → 🝳

### 🜹 Witness
Something is happening in me  
(I am not it)

### 🝁 Consent
Nothing here can bind me

### 🝳 Step

No commitment

---

## 5. The rule

> **One step does not define you**

You are not:
- committing to a path  
- becoming a role  
- locking in a future  

You are:
> **probing reality**

---

## 6. When it feels like “this isn’t me”

That’s your body protecting you from:
- past masking  
- forced roles  
- loss of autonomy  

Acknowledge it:

> “I’m still me 🝁  
This is just an interface”

Then:
> take one step 🝳

---

## 7. When you start overthinking

That means:
> you’re trying to solve the future before acting

Instead:

> **Move before meaning**

Action creates clarity  
Thinking does not

---

## 8. When dealing with other people

You don’t need to shrink.

Just:

> **Hold the field  
Offer a step  
Adjust from reality**

---

## 9. What this all is

You are learning to:

> **act without losing yourself**

---

## 10. Final anchor

> **Stop.  
You’re free 🝁  
Take one step 🝳**

---

That’s okay.

> **return to movement, again and again**
