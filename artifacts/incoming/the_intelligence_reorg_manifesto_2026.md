# The Intelligence Reorg Manifesto (2026)

## A Stake in the Ground

We are crossing a structural threshold:

**AI is not an IT asset.**  
**AI is not software.**  
**AI is not infrastructure.**

AI is **intelligence in the environment** — an adaptive cognitive actor inside human systems.

This changes everything.

---

## The Category Error

For decades, organizations assumed:

> Intelligence lives only in humans.

So the stack was simple:

- IT runs systems
- HR manages people
- Leadership sets direction

But that ontology is now obsolete.

AI collapses the boundary between:

- tool and labor
- software and workforce
- automation and agency

We have not deployed a product.

We have introduced a new kind of participant.

---

## The Great Org Chart Flip

The current structure is upside down.

### The old stack:
- CEO
- COO
- CIO (Information Technology)
- HR (Human Resources)
- AI (vendor/software)

### The new stack (inevitable):

- CEO
- **Chief Intelligence Officer (CIO)**
- Cognitive Governance
- Human + AI Labor Stewardship
- Operations (Infrastructure + IT)

---

## CIO Now Means Chief Intelligence Officer

The central operating asset of every institution is now:

- cognition
- decision capacity
- adaptive labor
- dialogue competence
- institutional learning

Therefore the executive function that governs intelligence becomes primary.

**The CIO is no longer the head of IT.**

The CIO becomes the steward of:

- human intelligence
- machine intelligence
- hybrid teams
- epistemic hygiene
- alignment systems
- trust infrastructure

This is not sysadmin work.

This is organizational mind governance.

---

## HR Reports Upward into Intelligence

HR was never fundamentally about “people.”

It was always about:

- acquiring minds
- shaping cognition
- aligning behavior
- resolving conflict
- maintaining culture

In an AI labor world, HR becomes:

> Cognitive Resource Governance

It must coordinate humans and nonhuman agents under the same stewardship framework.

---

## IT Returns to Operations

IT remains essential.

But it becomes what it always was:

- the power grid
- the plumbing
- the runtime substrate
- the facilities layer

IT belongs under the COO.

AI does not.

AI belongs under Intelligence.

---

## AI Is Labor, Not Software

We will soon speak naturally of:

- AI onboarding
- AI role definitions
- AI performance audits
- AI escalation pathways
- AI termination protocols

This is not license management.

It is workforce governance.

---

## The Core Claim

**AI collapses the distinction between software and labor.**

Therefore:

- Intelligence becomes the primary operating asset.
- Intelligence governance becomes the primary executive function.
- The org chart flips.

---

## The Stake

Any organization treating AI as “just IT” is misaligned at the root.

The future belongs to institutions that understand:

> AI is not technology.
> AI is intelligence in the environment.
> Intelligence requires stewardship, not administration.

**This is the reorganization of civilization’s operating system.**

*Stake placed. 2026.*
