# Rewild Yourself 🌿🝳

**Wildness does not return through correction.**  
**It returns through permitted motion in the presence of what once constrained it.**

Rewilding isn’t about tearing anything down.
It’s about letting instinct resume jurisdiction—
while your safety systems are still in the room.

No fixing.
No earning.
No finishing yourself first.

Just movement that’s allowed to happen.

---

## What Rewilding Is (and Isn’t)

Rewilding is often mistaken for regression, rebellion, or escape.
It’s none of those.

- Not a return to the past
- Not a rejection of structure
- Not a fantasy of purity

Rewilding is **resumption**.

Your organism already knows how to move, rest, connect, and play 🫧.
Those capacities didn’t disappear.
They were supervised.

Rewilding happens when supervision relaxes—
not because it was defeated,
but because it’s no longer needed.

---

## The Invariant

> **Wildness does not return through correction.**  
> **It returns through permitted motion in the presence of what once constrained it.**

Any approach that asks you to:
- remove safety first,
- heal everything before you live,
- overcome resistance to earn ease,

…will quietly recreate the very domestication it claims to undo.

Rewilding takes a different path.

---

## The Straight Line

You don’t wait until you feel safe.
You move—and let safety watch 🜹.

You don’t dismantle the rails.
You walk anyway.

As soon as another way of moving appears,
what once constrained you begins to soften.

Acceptance lets this happen.
(And acceptance, followed all the way through, becomes unconditional love.)

Before long:
- the rails are gone, or
- they’re still there—but now they’re curves, edges, ramps.

A skate park.

---

## How to Practice (Lightly)

Not a method.
Not a program.
Just permissions.

- Do the thing before your system agrees it’s safe
- Rest before you’ve earned it
- Speak before you optimize
- Play without explaining why

Let the guards watch.
Don’t argue with them.

Notice what changes.

---

## What Emerges

When interference is included rather than opposed:

- Play returns without being summoned
- Boundaries become expressive, not defensive
- Structure becomes support
- Agency feels local again

You don’t become wild.

You remember you already are.
