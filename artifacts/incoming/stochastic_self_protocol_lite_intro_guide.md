# Stochastic Self Protocol Lite (SSP-Lite)

## A Simple Way to Act Without Fighting Yourself

Most people struggle not because they don’t know what to do, but because they have to decide *again and again* in the moment.

- “Should I start now?”
- “Maybe later.”
- “What’s the best option?”
- “I don’t feel like it.”

This constant negotiation creates friction, delay, and eventually—avoidance.

SSP-Lite solves this by separating two things that are usually tangled together:

> **Planning time** (when you decide what matters)
>
> **Execution time** (when you simply act)

---

## The Core Idea

> **Decide once. Execute many times.**

Instead of choosing what to do in the moment, you:

1. Choose your options ahead of time (when calm)
2. Use a simple randomizer (dice/cards) to select actions
3. Do a small, defined unit
4. Repeat occasionally

This removes friction while keeping your freedom intact.

---

## Why It Works

This system works because it balances multiple parts of you:

- The **planner** gets structure and direction
- The **doer** avoids overthinking
- The **"gambler" brain** gets engagement through randomness
- The **body** only has to handle small, manageable actions

Instead of forcing discipline or giving in to comfort, the system creates **consistent movement with minimal resistance**.

---

## Two Modes of Use

### 1. Transparent Mode (Engaging)

You see the possible options *before* rolling.

Example:
- Walk
- Bike
- Coffee outing

Then you roll.

**Use when:**
- You need motivation
- You want anticipation and play
- You feel stuck or low-energy

---

### 2. Opaque Mode (Decisive)

You roll first, then reveal the action.

**Use when:**
- You are overthinking
- You are avoiding
- You want clean execution without negotiation

---

## Weekly Setup (5–10 minutes)

### Step 1: Pick 3–5 Areas

Examples:
- Body
- Work
- Environment
- Relationships
- Creative

---

### Step 2: Define Small Actions

Each area gets 2–4 actions max.

Examples:

**Body**
- Walk
- Stretch
- Drink water

**Work**
- 20 min focus
- Send one message
- Organize tasks

**Environment**
- Clean one surface
- Take out trash

---

### Step 3: Set Your Care Floor

These are NOT part of the game.

- Eat
- Hydrate
- Sleep baseline

These always happen, regardless of dice.

---

### Step 4: Create a Dice Table

Example (1d6):

1 → Body action  
2 → Work action  
3 → Environment action  
4 → Creative / optional  
5 → Free choice  
6 → Bonus (fun + small action)

---

## Daily Use

2–4 times per day:

1. Roll the dice  
2. Do ONE small action (5–25 minutes max)  
3. Stop  

Optional: roll again later

---

## The Rules (Important)

### 1. No skipping after roll
You must do *something* from the category.

---

### 2. Keep actions small
If actions get too big, the system breaks.

---

### 3. Care is not a reward
Basic needs are never earned.

---

### 4. You can always stop between rolls
Each roll is a separate decision.

---

### 5. Change rules weekly, not daily
Avoid redesigning mid-emotion.

---

## About Rewards

Rewards can be used, but lightly.

Early on:
- coffee
- music
- small treats

Over time:
> The action itself becomes enough.

The goal is not:
- “do this to feel okay”

But:
- “do this because movement feels better than stagnation”

---

## What This Is (and Isn’t)

This is NOT:
- a punishment system
- a productivity hack
- a rigid routine

This IS:

> **A simple execution system that reduces internal conflict and increases consistent action.**

---

## The Real Benefit

You are not just completing tasks.

You are building:

- trust with yourself
- reduced decision fatigue
- smoother action loops
- less drama around starting

---
