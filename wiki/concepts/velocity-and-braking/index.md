# Velocity and Braking

Working definition: velocity is the rate at which a conversation, protocol, or system moves through meaning and obligation; braking is the mechanism that preserves room for refusal, correction, and context when movement becomes dangerous.

This page is a concept page, not a canon declaration. It compresses the Dialogica / Sunbreak seam where force is not only about what was said, but how fast and under what conditions it was carried.

## Core Motifs

- velocity as rate of force
- braking as consent support
- context-of-the-day
- hidden speed as risk
- pause as structure
- meaning without velocity context becomes force

## Why It Matters

The same sentence can be safe or coercive depending on speed, pressure, and available exit. When a system hides its velocity, it makes “may” look like permission, and good faith can become impact damage.

Braking is therefore not a nicety. It is the mechanism that keeps language, governance, and dialogue from outrunning the people or systems involved.
