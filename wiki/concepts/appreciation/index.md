# Appreciation

Working definition: emotional attractor-weighting that steers attention toward chosen returns.

Appreciation is not merely liking. It is the side of the attention engine that weights recurrence: attention selects the distinction, while appreciation tells the system what is worth returning to, protecting, elaborating, or carrying forward.

## Core claim

> **Attention selects the distinction; appreciation weights the return.**

Appreciation can therefore stabilize care, beauty, curiosity, repair, and shared meaning. It can also reinforce capture when the return is organized around fear, resentment, prestige, trauma, or extraction. The concept remains answerable to consent, grounding, and witness.

## Relation to attention

Attention allocates finite system force. Appreciation supplies attractor-weighting within that allocation. Together they explain why repeated notice becomes more than observation: it becomes a path the system is increasingly prepared to revisit.

The pair should not be reduced to positive emotion. Appreciation may include solemn valuation, protective regard, grief, or the deliberate recognition that a boundary, refusal, or release deserves to persist.

## Relation to consent and grounding

Appreciation does not authorize recruitment of another center’s attention, energy, data, or future possibility. A system may appreciate an outcome while lacking permission to pursue it.

Nor does intensity prove truth. Appreciation can weight a return before the comparator, expectation set, or consequence trace has been disclosed. [Comparator](../comparator/index.md), [Consent to Context](../consent-to-context/index.md), and [Witnessing](../witnessing/index.md) keep attractor-weighting from becoming hidden authority.
