# Semantic Lineage

This branch collects the work on semantic lineage, semantic dynamics, semantic thermodynamics, and the lineage-markup grammar that keeps them witnessable in the wiki.

It is the project-level home for the insight that meaning has a history of position, not just a history of text. The field changes around artifacts even when the artifacts remain unchanged, so the wiki needs a way to keep provenance, comparison, and recontextualization visible without erasing the older record.

## Current Shape

- 2 published essays.
- 1 concept page.
- 2 lineage-support sources archived from the originating conversation.

## Working Read

This is the semantic-history branch for the corpus. It treats a body of work as something that can change position within a field of meaning even when the source artifact itself remains fixed.

The published essays carry the theory:
- [When Meaning Begins to Move](../../external/shimmerymemory/essays/when-meaning-begins-to-move/index.md) develops semantic lineage, semantic dynamics, and semantic thermodynamics.
- [The Motion of Meaning](../../external/shimmerymemory/essays/the-motion-of-meaning/index.md) frames semantic complementarity, semantic gravity, and the measurement problem for meaning.

The concept page gives the visualization vocabulary:
- [Semantic Doppler](../../concepts/semantic-doppler/index.md) renders semantic motion relative to a selected context.

The transcript pair preserves the wiki-specific lineage grammar that made the broader project legible in the first place.
