## Working Read

This family makes one sequence explicit: witness first, orchestration second, execution last.

The product overview frames the platform as a visibility-first operational layer. The orchestration documents then show how authority, policy text, and event-driven execution can remain legible when AI participates. The witness document keeps the stack from collapsing too quickly into automation by preserving a read-only, role-aware mirror as the first durable move.
