## Working Read

This note frames consent-scoped communication as infrastructure: a missing layer beneath many tools rather than a standalone social product. The wedge is AI-augmented teams that need safe summarization and scoped memory.

## Core Claim

The business opportunity is in replacing fragmented communication modes with one explicit-scope substrate that can scale from open collaboration to hyper-secure environments.

## Key Ideas

- Explicit scope is the missing layer.
- Public data should be genuinely public.
- The protocol works across one handshake ladder.
- AI turns implicit boundaries into a liability.
