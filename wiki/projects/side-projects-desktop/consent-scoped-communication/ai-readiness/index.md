## Working Read

The note argues that AI readiness starts with machine-readable intent: inventory data surfaces, assign guardianship, and gate human and automated access through explicit purpose-bound policy.

## Core Claim

If intent is not machine-readable, AI will treat access as permission. Structural clarity is the first safety step.

## Key Ideas

- Machine-readable intent is the missing layer.
- Permissions and cultural norms are not enough.
- Guardianship and purpose statements must be explicit.
- AI capability without constraint becomes liability.
