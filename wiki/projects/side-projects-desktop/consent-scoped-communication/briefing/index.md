The briefing is a constraint guide for public-facing material: keep the tone observational, center the structural failure mode, and avoid drifting into explanation, hype, or product talk.

## Core Claim

Content should reinforce the idea that context without consent becomes toxic debt and that consent must be structurally honored rather than retrofitted after learning.

## Key Ideas

- Dead-pan delivery is strategic.
- Context without consent becomes toxic debt.
- Tone must stay calm and observational.
- The goal is to help people notice the problem more clearly.
