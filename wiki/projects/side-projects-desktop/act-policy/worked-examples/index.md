# Worked Examples

This seam holds the demonstrative examples for the ACT–POLICY substrate.

The examples show that ordinary interaction, mediation, and witness handling can all reduce to ACTs evaluated by POLICY, with logging and consent remaining explicit rather than implied.

## Working Read

The examples cover no-witness information exchange, witness invitation and acceptance, policy binding by primaries, witness-enforced violation handling, consented logging and readback, and round-robin witness rotation. The final mapping to Dialogica shows how the same substrate can frame mediation without inventing new primitives.

This page should remain a demonstrator, not a separate doctrine. Its job is to keep the substrate legible in use.
