## Working Read

Shimmerflow is framed as a nested-reality exploration game where progress is measured by coherence, witnessing, remembering, and perspective shift rather than conquest. The player can move between avatar, environment, flow, and substrate layers, so the design language treats agency as a change in lens rather than a flat sequence of wins.

The Triloop integration note sharpens that into architecture. `LoopLang` governs truth and consent mechanics, `LoopStory` governs symbolic memory and emotional recursion, and `LoopGame` governs play and feedback. Together they keep the project from collapsing into either a linear story or a merely procedural game.
