## Working Read

The manual gives the ethical rule set: entrain domains, not people; declare scope up front; preserve reversibility; keep sovereign domains out of scope unless explicitly opted in. The protocol turns that into a single portable file that can carry vision, action, review, and ethical constraint without losing history.

The snapshot note reframes rhythm, resonance, and entrainment as a phase-aware substrate for coordination, while the charts-to-flows orientation widens that picture into trajectories, anchors, and high-dimensional movement. Taken together, the branch reads as a practical grammar for coordination that refuses to collapse into coercion.
