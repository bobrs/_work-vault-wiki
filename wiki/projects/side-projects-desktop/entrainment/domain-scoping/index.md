# Domain Scoping

This seam holds the ethical manual for domain-scoped entrainment.

The page treats entrainment as a selective coordination practice, not a global capture mechanism. The central move is to define what can synchronize, what must stay sovereign, and how participants can leave without penalty.

## Working Read

The manual is explicit that we do not entrain people; we entrain domains. That distinction keeps time, attention, process, and behavior available for coordination while protecting belief, identity, moral conscience, and personal meaning unless they are deliberately and reversibly invited in.

This is the ethical half of the entrainment seam. It belongs here because its job is to explain how rhythm and ritual can be powerful without becoming coercive.
