# The Telic Field Papers

Status: candidate  
Source role: semantic wiki layer  
Processing tier: 4  
Content canon status: unset

This branch collects the source, vocabulary, lineage, conceptual papers, and implementation work that emerged from the 2026-07-13 conversation on attractors, teleology, telic fields, constitutional selves, context, time, and semantic polytelometry.

It is a lineage container, not a canon declaration.

The branch exists because a durable seam became visible across several existing bodies of work:

- dyads and standing;
- consent and intention compression;
- witness and provenance;
- semantic collapse and semantic integrity;
- loop mechanics and repair;
- fractal identity and internal multiplicity;
- temporal continuity;
- comparators and correction;
- local, consent-bound AI.

The recovered 2026-07-12 origin transcript is now the primary witnessed source. It was extracted from the verified ChatGPT export into a user-visible Markdown record; its repository path, hash, artifact ID, and public/private disposition are recorded on the [Sources](sources.md) page. The raw export remains a local/private forensic reference because it contains internal and tool-execution material. The derived session compression, glossary, lineage map, and candidate concept pages should not outrank the transcript.
