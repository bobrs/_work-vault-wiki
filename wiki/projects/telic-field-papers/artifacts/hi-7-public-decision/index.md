## Working focus

HI-7 completes I.7 and H.7 as a phase-locked pass. It preserves public plurality without inventing one collective mind; treats standing as prior to counting; makes option construction and omission visible; distinguishes protected conditions from weighted preferences; exposes decision-method plurality; preserves minority standing and dissent; records route portfolios, abstention, no-decision states, cost and delay bearers; and returns observed consequence to public navigation.

The package reports twelve candidate schemas, eight positive demonstrations, twelve negative conformance fixtures, an event-generated PublicDecisionWitness, an independent public-decision export, and zero technical/public contradictions. These are structural and internal semantic validations, not legal standing determinations, a universal democratic rule, fairness proof, production security, scientific validity, or public-reader comprehension.

## Gate and boundaries

HI-7 is complete at v0.1 with a **PASS WITH CONDITIONS** gate. Remaining conditions include standing capture, option flooding, protected-condition inflation, method shopping, portfolio burden dumping, dissent privacy, selective consequence reporting, and independent public-reader testing.

The I.7 specification, H.7 page, matrices, schemas, demonstrations, fixtures, and export remain working/candidate artifacts. Installation does not make them canonical or production specifications.
