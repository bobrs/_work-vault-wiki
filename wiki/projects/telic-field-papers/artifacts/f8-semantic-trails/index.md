## Working focus

F.8 distinguishes telic terrain, action and consequence, persistent trace, later uptake, and semantic trail. It defines a semantic field narrowly as a governed ecology of durable traces and interpretive practices, and develops trace versus meaning, semantic persistence and integrity, witness and provenance, reactivation, drift, inheritance, shared fields, stigmergic trails, distributed memory, semantic commons and enclosure, knowledge graphs, model participation, breach, repair, forgetting, and release.

The candidate Semantic Trail Record tracks source and affected centers, evidence status, telic function, scope, authority, provenance transformations, integrity, uptake, corrections, forks, derived trails, and lifecycle state. The included W3C PROV mapping extends existing provenance concepts rather than proposing a parallel provenance substrate.

F.8 remains a candidate paper. The record and provenance mapping are research artifacts, not production specifications, and do not become canonical by installation.
