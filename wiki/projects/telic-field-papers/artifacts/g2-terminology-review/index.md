## Working finding

G.2 does not claim an empty vocabulary space or settled novelty. It places the Telic Field Papers beside teleological centers of life, telic systems, telic/paratelic states, linguistic telicity, polytely, telic states, telic controllability, enactivism, dialogical self theory, cybernetics, and multiobjective decision theory.

The candidate contribution remains the composition of:

- centers of standing and localized end-relative significance;
- relations that create possibilities without erasing local fields;
- scoped and corrigible projections;
- consent as governance of recruitment into ends;
- witness and provenance across time;
- context capacity as a limit on active standing;
- polytelometric navigation before optimization;
- semantic comparison without model sovereignty.

## Terminology dispositions

- Retain telic field, with mandatory distinction from neighboring uses such as telic state and telic controllability.
- Adopt telic projection estimation for technical work; preserve simplified telic field calculation in lineage only.
- Retain polytelometry, explicitly distinguishing it from polytely and multiobjective optimization.
- Retain semantic polytelometry as a candidate capability class.
- Retain constitutional self, context carrying capacity, and temporal standing with their stated boundaries.
- Prefer telic integrity in technical writing.
- Preserve teleological graft rejection as evocative lineage language, not preferred technical terminology.
