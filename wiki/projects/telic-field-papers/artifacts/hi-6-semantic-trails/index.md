## Working focus

HI-6 completes I.6 and H.6 as a phase-locked pass. It keeps records distinct from living events and traces distinct from fields; treats memory as reconstruction; separates active memory from archive; preserves source, inference, and correction boundaries; propagates correction into retrieval; distinguishes salience from legitimacy; supports decay and protected forgetting; enables stigmergic coordination; bounds model-session memory; makes cross-loop transfer visible; and quarantines stale authority.

The package reports twelve candidate schemas, eight positive demonstrations, twelve negative conformance fixtures, an event-generated SemanticTrailWitness, an independent semantic-trail export, and zero technical/public contradictions. These are structural and internal semantic validations, not a complete theory of memory, legal deletion compliance, guaranteed descendant deletion, production security, scientific validity, or public-reader comprehension.

## Gate and boundaries

HI-6 is complete at v0.1 with a **PASS WITH CONDITIONS** gate. Remaining conditions include source-independence tracking, salience manipulation, correction fan-out, deletion verification, archive leakage, model relationship inflation, and independent public-reader testing.

The I.6 specification, H.6 page, matrices, schemas, demonstrations, fixtures, and export remain working/candidate artifacts. Installation does not make them canonical or production specifications.
