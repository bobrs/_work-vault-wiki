## Working focus

F.6 defines temporal standing as the requirement that temporally displaced states or successors remain represented in present decisions that materially constrain the conditions they will inherit. It develops continuity without perfect sameness, present authority as temporary governance, future possibility as a protected condition, commitment as temporal recruitment, inherited-authority review, versioned consent, temporal witness, identity as a continuity protocol, succession, release from prior teloi, temporal breach, and repair.

The candidate Temporal Standing Record tracks valid time, recording time, prior authority, future affected centers, preserved and closed possibilities, irreversible effects, commitments, present/future/successor standing, consent and authority versions, uncertainties, review triggers, release conditions, and temporal lifecycle status.

The record remains a research schema rather than a production specification. F.6 remains a candidate paper and does not become canonical by installation.
