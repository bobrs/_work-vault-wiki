## Working focus

F.4 replaces the lineage phrase `simplified telic field calculation` with `telic projection estimation` for current technical work. It distinguishes field, projection, and receiver mirror, and treats every consequential system as acting on a scoped, lossy, uncertain, and corrigible representation rather than direct access to a complete field.

The paper establishes evidence classes, separate descriptive/interpretive/action authority, multidimensional uncertainty, confidence without authority, expiration, revocation, privacy, correction, projection integrity, breach taxonomy, receiver responsibility, and a minimum Telic Projection Record.

The included record remains a research schema and does not become a Phase I production specification by installation.

F.4 remains a candidate paper and does not become canonical by installation.
