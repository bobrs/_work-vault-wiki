## Working finding

G.6 grounds context carrying capacity beside Ashby’s requisite variety and the good-regulator theorem, bounded rationality, working memory, cognitive load, information bottleneck theory, distributed cognition, common ground, organizational information processing, sensemaking, long-context language-model evaluation, retrieval, compression, and graceful degradation.

The review identifies the candidate addition as standing-aware capacity: preservation of relevant plurality, authority, privacy, provenance, uncertainty, correction, and stop conditions. It requires these additions to remain empirically distinguishable from neighboring concepts rather than treating context carrying capacity as a renamed total-capacity score.

G.6 remains a research and claim-boundary artifact. The capacity profile is not a universal intelligence score, human-worth ranking, automatic discrimination mechanism, justification for total context collection, or replacement for domain-specific assessment.
