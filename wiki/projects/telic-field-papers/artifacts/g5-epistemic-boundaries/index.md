## Working finding

G.5 places telic projection estimation beside requirements elicitation, intent recognition, user modeling, preference learning, inverse reinforcement learning, theory of mind, construct validity, calibration, clinical formulation, and data minimization.

The review separates accuracy from validity, confidence from authority, available information from interpretive permission, and record deletion from model unlearning. It retains source authority as privileged self-description without infallibility, requires action authority to be separately grounded, and treats observed behavior and model inference as evidence rather than transparent access to preference.

G.5 establishes projection expiration, revocation, correction, inferential privacy, multidimensional uncertainty, and minimum-necessary projection as boundary requirements. It remains a research and claim-boundary artifact, not a production specification.
