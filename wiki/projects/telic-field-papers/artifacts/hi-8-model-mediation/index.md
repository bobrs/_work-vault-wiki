## Working focus

HI-8 completes I.8 and H.8 as a phase-locked pass. It treats models as semantic navigators rather than sovereign centers; distinguishes maps as projections of projections; labels source, inference, summary, generation, recommendation, and authorization; preserves standing through compression; retains minority fields; exposes option authorship and agenda power; detects sycophancy and synthetic consensus; discloses provider purpose; preserves model disagreement; blocks recommendations at the action boundary; and makes correction and refusal operative.

The package reports twelve candidate schemas, eight positive demonstrations, twelve negative conformance fixtures, an event-generated ModelMediatedDecisionWitness, an independent model-mediated export, and zero technical/public contradictions. These are structural and internal semantic validations, not proof of model neutrality, complete source fidelity, provider transparency, production safety, legal authority, scientific validity, or public-reader comprehension.
