## Working focus

FG-S consolidates the F.1–F.11 foundation sequence and G.1–G.12 adjacent-fields review into a constitutional semantic spine:

```text
center → source → projection → semantic trail → active context → receiver mirror
→ field map → route → governance gate → authorized action → consequence → witness
→ correction, release, succession, or dissolution
```

The package freezes and qualifies the working terminology, audits thirty cross-paper tensions, consolidates eight candidate record families around a common envelope, classifies ninety-six claims, and recommends a controlled parallel HI-0 opening: I.0 technical semantic spine and core envelope specification alongside H.0 public reader architecture.

The freeze remains qualified rather than canonical. `telic field` remains the technical root; standing is typed; semantic field is scoped; polytelometry remains provisional and non-scalar; semantic polytelometry is an architecture rather than model mind reading; training authority is broader than consent; and consentfully trained is a lineage profile rather than a badge.

## Status and boundaries

FG-S is complete as a v0.1 working bridge pass. It does not canonize the series, settle every open hypothesis, or turn the candidate record envelope into a production specification. H.0 and I.0 should begin with three worked examples and a gate review before deeper H.1 or I.1 branching.
