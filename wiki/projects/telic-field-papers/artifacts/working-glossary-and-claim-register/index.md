## Working read

This artifact stabilizes the vocabulary that emerged during the origin conversation while keeping uncertainty attached to the terms.

It separates inherited terms, extended terms, candidate-coined terms, working definitions, structural hypotheses, ethical claims, implementation proposals, metaphors, and open questions.

Its purpose is not to settle the language. Its purpose is to prevent useful language from becoming more certain than the structures it was created to witness.

## Core claim

> **Do not let a useful term become more certain than the structure it was created to witness.**

## Drift controls

The glossary explicitly prevents:

- treating “field” as established physics;
- treating coherence as proof;
- treating internal parts as externally sovereign persons;
- treating projection as diagnosis;
- assuming that more context is always better;
- collapsing protected conditions into optimizable objectives;
- rewriting earlier sources with later terms.
