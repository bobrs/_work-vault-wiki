## Working focus

HI-5 completes I.5 and H.5 as a phase-locked pass. It distinguishes legitimate, visible, bounded, reciprocal, reviewable, and escapable dependency from hidden recruitment; tests compatibility against constitutional substrate rather than copied form; treats material mission drift as requiring reauthorization; keeps maintenance burden visible; and defines lock-in, capture, effective withdrawal, governed fork, dissolution, residual obligations, and record custody.

The package reports twelve candidate schemas, eight positive demonstrations, twelve negative conformance fixtures, an event-generated DependencyWitness, an independent dependency export, and zero technical/public contradictions. These are structural and internal semantic validations, not clinical assessment, legal findings, production security, economic proportionality, scientific validity, or public-reader comprehension.

## Gate and boundaries

HI-5 is complete at v0.1 with a **PASS WITH CONDITIONS** gate. Remaining conditions include hidden-support discovery, qualitative burden assessment, strategic withdrawal, false-drift safeguards, exit externalities, dissolution capture, and independent public-reader testing.

The I.5 specification, H.5 page, matrices, schemas, demonstrations, fixtures, and export remain working/candidate artifacts. Installation does not make them canonical or production specifications.
