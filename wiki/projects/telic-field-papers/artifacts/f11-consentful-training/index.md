## Working focus

F.11 defines consentfully trained models as a qualified training-lineage profile rather than a binary moral badge. It treats training as outer-loop recruitment and develops source and community standing, training authority, transition-specific provenance and restrictions, dataset documentation, human labor, preference provenance, RLHF/DPO/RLAIF, model constitutions, withdrawal and unlearning truthfulness, synthetic-data lineage, benefit governance, model succession, and separate training and operation profiles.

The lineage record tracks source collections, authority basis, provenance transformations, human contributions, preference governance, constitutions, training events, synthetic-data recursion, withdrawal and unlearning evidence, succession, benefits, accountability, and lifecycle status.

F.11 remains a candidate paper. Consentful operation does not retroactively make unconsented training consentful; consentful training does not guarantee consentful deployment. The record and profiles remain research artifacts, not production specifications, and do not become canonical by installation.
