## Revision status

F.1 v0.2 is a preserved revision of the F.1 v0.1 predecessor. It incorporates the G.1 and G.2 reviews without silently overwriting the earlier version.

The revision:

- distinguishes telic fields from telic states, telic controllability, telic systems, paratelic states, linguistic telicity, and teleological centers of life;
- adopts telic projection estimation for technical use;
- preserves simplified telic field calculation as lineage language;
- narrows novelty claims to a candidate composition of known problem families;
- protects paratelic and atelic value, including play, presence, rest, and release from inherited purpose;
- deepens standing, context carrying capacity, temporal standing, constitutional-self, and semantic-polytelometry boundaries.
