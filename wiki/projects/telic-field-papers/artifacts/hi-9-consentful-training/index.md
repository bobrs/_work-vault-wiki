## Working focus

HI-9 completes I.9 and H.9 as a phase-locked pass. It treats training as a recruitment relation; distinguishes public availability, access, permission, consent, ownership, and legitimacy; makes authority transition-specific; preserves source and community standing through transformation; retains annotation and preference provenance; exposes model constitutions; preserves synthetic ancestry; governs withdrawal and bounded unlearning; propagates obligations to derivative models; connects benefit sharing to contribution; and separates training legitimacy from deployment authority.

The package reports twelve candidate schemas, eight positive demonstrations, twelve negative conformance fixtures, an event-generated ConsentfulTrainingWitness, an independent training-lineage export, and zero technical/public contradictions. These are structural and internal semantic validations, not legal conclusions, complete provenance, exact machine unlearning, universal community authority, sufficient compensation, production security, scientific validity, or public-reader comprehension.

## Gate and boundaries

HI-9 is complete at v0.1 with a **PASS WITH CONDITIONS** gate. Remaining conditions include incomplete provenance, community-authority capture, restriction loss through mixing, preference universalization, unlearning theater, successor opacity, benefit washing, and independent public-reader testing.

The I.9 specification, H.9 page, matrices, schemas, demonstrations, fixtures, and export remain working/candidate artifacts. Installation does not make them canonical or production specifications.
