## Working focus

F.2 develops the dyad as the minimum analytic relation through which two locally organized telic fields become mutually consequential. It treats the dyad as an analytic cut, not a proven universal ontological atom.

The paper addresses:

- local fields, projections, receiver mirrors, and relational fields;
- emergent purpose without automatic sovereignty;
- witness as functional thirdness;
- higher loops and lower-loop standing;
- projection, context, constitutional, and relational failure;
- repair, refusal, and a viable route home to presence.

F.2 remains a candidate paper and does not become canonical by installation.
