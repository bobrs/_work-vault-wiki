## Working finding

G.4 places the constitutional-self proposal beside dialogical self theory, Internal Family Systems, ego-state and modes traditions, structural dissociation, predictive processing, embodiment, self-distancing, and relational formation. It separates therapeutic usefulness from proof of literal internal ontology and keeps memory, clinical, and AI boundaries explicit.

The review establishes person-level sovereignty, user-controlled vocabulary, source/inference separation, ontology neutrality, non-diagnostic operation, memory discipline, expiration, dependence monitoring, stabilization, local custody, and human handoff as safety requirements. AI may organize user-authored reflection, but may not diagnose, declare hidden entities real, infer recovered memories, bypass consent, or serve as the sole crisis resource.

G.4 remains a research and claim-boundary artifact, not a clinical protocol or production specification.
