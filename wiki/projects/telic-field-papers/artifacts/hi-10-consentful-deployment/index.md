## Working focus

HI-10 completes I.10 and H.10 and closes the candidate H/I sequence. It treats deployment as a new field; admits runtime standing without training contribution; separates capability, role, tool access, and authority; governs notice, consent, refusal, runtime data recruitment, purpose drift, meaningful review, affected-center repair, consequence monitoring, operator/provider transfer, model succession, rollback, retirement, and residual obligations.

The package reports twelve candidate schemas, eight positive demonstrations, twelve negative conformance fixtures, an event-generated ConsentfulDeploymentWitness, an independent deployment-witness export, and zero technical/public contradictions. These are structural and internal semantic validations, not legal authority, consent sufficiency, clinical safety, cybersecurity, complete compensation, complete residual-state deletion, scientific validity, or public-reader comprehension.

## Gate and boundaries

HI-10 is complete at v0.1 with a **PASS WITH CONDITIONS** gate. Remaining conditions include runtime-standing scale, tool-token overreach, consent fragmentation, cumulative drift, incident minimization, dynamic model-version opacity, retirement abandonment, and independent public-reader testing.

The I.10 specification, H.10 page, matrices, schemas, demonstrations, fixtures, and export remain working/candidate artifacts. Installation does not make them canonical or production specifications.
